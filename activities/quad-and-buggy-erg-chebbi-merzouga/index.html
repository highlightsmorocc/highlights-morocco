Overview

Quad and Buggy in Merzouga

A quad and buggy ride in Erg Chebbi, Merzouga, lasts one hour. Prices start from €50 for 2 people and from €40 for 1 person riding solo, on a quad or a buggy. You go with a local guide across the dunes near Merzouga.

Quad bikes and buggies are the fast way to see the dunes. In one hour you cover ground that would take much longer on foot or by camel, with sand flying and the horizon wide open.

You can ride together in one vehicle, from €50 for 2 people, or drive solo, from €40 for 1 person, on a quad or on a buggy. Choose a quad for a bike-style ride, or a buggy for a seat with more room and a rollcage.

A local guide leads the way and shows you the best tracks. Many travellers pair this with a sunset camel ride or the 4x4 excursion with the nomads, to see the desert at different speeds.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much is a quad or buggy ride in Merzouga?

From €50 for 2 people and from €40 for 1 person riding solo, on a quad or a buggy. Each ride lasts one hour.

How long does the ride last?

One hour. If you would like to ride longer, ask us when you contact us and we will tell you the options.

Quad or buggy: which should I choose?

A quad is a bike-style ride and is great for one person. A buggy has a seat, more room and a rollcage. If you are two people and want to ride together, ask us what is available.

Can I drive myself?

Yes. The price from €40 is for 1 person riding solo, on a quad or a buggy. A guide leads the way so you do not get lost in the dunes.

Can I combine it with a camel trek?

Yes. Many travellers ride a quad or buggy for an hour and add a camel ride at sunset. We can plan both for the same day.

What should I bring?

Closed shoes, long trousers, a scarf or face covering, and sunglasses or goggles. Sand and dust fly up on the dunes.
