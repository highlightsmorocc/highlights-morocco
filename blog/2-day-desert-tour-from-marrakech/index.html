The short answer

Yes, you can do a desert tour from Marrakech in 2 days. A private 2-day tour crosses the High Atlas, visits Ait Ben Haddou and Ouarzazate and reaches the Sahara at Merzouga, about 560 km away, or at Zagora, about 350 km away. You spend one night in a desert camp, with a camel ride, dinner and breakfast, and return to Marrakech on day two. Choose Merzouga for the tallest dunes and Zagora for a shorter drive. The price is on request, with a free quote and no obligation.

Many travellers have only a few days in Morocco and still want a night in the Sahara. A 2-day tour makes this possible, but it is a trip with long driving days, so it is important to know what to expect and to choose the right destination. This guide compares Merzouga and Zagora, gives the route and the itinerary of each, explains what is included and whether two days are enough, and tells you how to book. The 2-day tour to Merzouga

Day 1: Marrakech to Merzouga (about 560 km)

Leave Marrakech early with your private driver and local guide. Cross the High Atlas by the Tizi n'Tichka pass, with photo stops, visit Ait Ben Haddou and stop in Ouarzazate. Continue through the Dades Valley to Merzouga, and watch the sunset over the Erg Chebbi dunes. Have dinner and spend the night in a desert camp.

Day 2: Merzouga to Marrakech (about 560 km)

Wake up to the sunrise over the dunes and have breakfast at camp. Say goodbye to the desert and drive back to Marrakech with stops for lunch and rest. You arrive in the evening, at the point we agree with you. See the full 2-day Marrakech to Merzouga tour.

The 2-day tour to Zagora

Day 1: Marrakech to Zagora (about 350 km)

Leave Marrakech in the morning, cross the Tizi n'Tichka pass and visit Ait Ben Haddou and Ouarzazate. Follow the palm groves of the Draa Valley, the longest river valley in Morocco, to Zagora, ride a camel at sunset and spend the night in a desert camp with dinner and breakfast.

Day 2: Zagora to Marrakech (about 350 km)

Watch the sunrise over the desert, have breakfast and stop at the famous sign to Timbuktu in Zagora before you leave. Drive back to Marrakech by the same mountain road, with stops. See the full 2-day Marrakech to Zagora tour.

Is a desert tour in 2 days enough?

Two days are enough to see the Sahara and spend a night in a camp, but the trip is demanding. On the Merzouga route you spend about 8 to 10 hours in the vehicle on each of the two days, with stops, and on the Zagora route the driving is shorter. If you are travelling with children, if you prefer a relaxed pace or if you want more time in the dunes, a 3-day tour is a better choice, with an extra night to rest, a sunrise and more activities. A 3-day tour also adds the Todra Gorges and a night in the Dades Valley. You can compare the options in the guide to Sahara excursions from Marrakech.

Day 1 hour by hour (Merzouga route)

  • 7:00: pick-up in Marrakech and the climb to the Tizi n'Tichka pass.
  • 9:30: photo stops on the mountain road, with views of the High Atlas.
  • 11:30: Ait Ben Haddou, with time to cross the old bridge and climb to the top.
  • 13:30: lunch in or near Ouarzazate.
  • 15:00: the road through the Dades Valley and the plains of the south-east.
  • 17:30: arrival at Merzouga and the sunset over the Erg Chebbi dunes.
  • 20:00: dinner, music and a night in the camp.

These times are an example, and we adjust the start time and the stops to your group. An early start is the key to enjoying the day. The Zagora route follows the same pattern with a shorter drive, so you arrive earlier and have more time at the camp.

The night in the desert camp

After the sunset, you reach the camp, usually on foot or by camel. The camp has tents with beds, a dining tent and a place to sit around the fire. Dinner is served, often with a tagine, and there may be drums and songs under the stars. The sky is very clear, so bring a jacket and look up. In the morning you can climb a dune to watch the sunrise, then have breakfast before you leave. The type of camp and the facilities are confirmed in your quote, and we can arrange a higher comfort level if you prefer. See our luxury Morocco tours guide and the overnight desert camp.

What is included in the 2-day tour

  • Private transport: a vehicle with a driver for your group only.
  • A local guide: a guide who speaks English or Spanish.
  • A camel ride: a ride in the dunes, at sunset.
  • A night in a desert camp: a private tent, dinner and breakfast.
  • Pick-up and drop-off: from your hotel or riad in Marrakech.
  • Support: help before and during your trip.

Lunches, drinks, tips and entrance fees are usually not included. We confirm every detail, such as the type of camp and the meals, in your free quote.

What to bring

  • A small night bag: keep your large bag in the vehicle.
  • Layers: desert evenings and mornings are cold, even after a warm day.
  • A scarf: to protect your face from sun and dust.
  • Sunglasses and sun cream: the light is very strong.
  • Closed shoes: for the dunes and the kasbahs.
  • Water and snacks: the days are long.
  • A phone charger: for the car, and a power bank for the camp.

For more advice, read our Morocco travel tips.

Best time for a 2-day desert tour

Spring and autumn are the best seasons, with warm days and cool nights. Summer is very hot in the south, so we plan early starts and long breaks, and a night in the desert can still be pleasant. In winter, the days are mild but the nights are cold, and snow can fall in the High Atlas, which can change the road. See the best time to visit Morocco.

If you have more time

If you can add a day or two, the Sahara is much easier to enjoy. A 3-day tour from Marrakech gives you the Todra Gorges, a night in the Dades Valley and a sunrise. If you want to end in a different city, the 3-day Marrakech to Fes desert tour crosses the country and finishes in Fes. For a longer journey, see the Morocco itineraries.

Common mistakes with a 2-day desert tour

  • Expecting a relaxed trip: two days to Merzouga means long drives; accept it or choose 3 days.
  • Booking the cheapest option without checking: ask about the vehicle, the camp and what is included.
  • Overpacking: a small night bag is much easier in the camp.
  • Forgetting warm clothes: the desert is cold at night, even in spring.
  • Planning a flight the same night: leave a buffer, because roads can be slower than expected.
  • Skipping the research: read our guide on what to check before booking a Sahara tour.

Who a 2-day desert tour is for

  • Short trips: travellers with a layover or a few days in Marrakech.
  • First-time visitors: people who want to see the Sahara once, at least for a night.
  • Couples and friends: a night under the stars with a campfire.
  • Photographers: a sunset and a sunrise on the dunes.
  • Not ideal for: people who dislike long drives or who travel with very young children; choose a longer tour.

2-day desert tour from Marrakech: frequently asked questions

Can you do a desert tour from Marrakech in 2 days?

Yes. A private 2-day desert tour from Marrakech takes you over the High Atlas to Merzouga or Zagora, with a night in a desert camp, and brings you back to Marrakech on day two. The days are long, because the distances are large.

How far is Merzouga from Marrakech?

Merzouga is about 560 km from Marrakech each way, so a 2-day tour is a trip for people who enjoy the road. If you prefer a slower pace, a 3 or 4-day tour is more relaxed.

How far is Zagora from Marrakech?

Zagora is about 350 km from Marrakech each way. It is closer than Merzouga, so the drive is shorter, and it is a good choice for a 2-day tour.

Merzouga or Zagora for a 2-day tour?

For the tall golden dunes of Erg Chebbi choose Merzouga, and for a shorter drive, palm groves and smaller dunes choose Zagora. In 2 days, Zagora is less tiring and Merzouga is more spectacular.

What is included in the 2-day desert tour?

A private driver, a local guide who speaks English or Spanish, a camel ride and a night in a desert camp with dinner and breakfast. Lunches, drinks and entrance fees are usually not included, and we confirm the details in the quote.

Is the 2-day tour private?

Yes. Our tours are private, with your own vehicle, driver and guide. You choose the pick-up point and the pace.

What do I see on the way?

On both routes you cross the High Atlas by the Tizi n'Tichka pass, visit Ait Ben Haddou and stop in Ouarzazate. The Merzouga route continues through the Dades Valley, and the Zagora route follows the Draa Valley.

Is it worth doing a desert tour in only 2 days?

Yes, if your time is short. You see the Atlas, a UNESCO ksar, a landscape of kasbahs and a night in the Sahara. If you can, 3 days give you more time and less driving each day.

How much does the 2-day desert tour cost?

The price is on request, because it depends on your dates and the number of travellers. We send a free quote with no obligation.

What is the best time of year for a desert tour?

Spring and autumn are the most comfortable. Summer is very hot, and winter nights in the desert are cold, so bring warm layers.
