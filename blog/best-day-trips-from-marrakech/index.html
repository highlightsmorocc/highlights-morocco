The short answer

The best day trips from Marrakech are the Ourika Valley, the Ouzoud Waterfalls, Ait Ben Haddou with the Ouarzazate region, Essaouira, the Agafay desert and the Atlas Mountains. Ourika and Agafay are the closest, about an hour away, while Ouzoud, Essaouira and Ait Ben Haddou make a longer day. Our private day trips cover Ourika, Ouzoud and Ait Ben Haddou, with transport from Marrakech, a local guide on request and a return the same day.

Marrakech is a perfect base for day trips. In a few hours you can leave the heat of the city for a green valley, a tall waterfall, a UNESCO-listed ksar, an Atlantic port or a stony desert. This guide compares the main options, tells you how far each is, who it suits and how to choose, and explains how to book a private trip. When you are ready, message us on WhatsApp for a free quote.

Ourika Valley

The Ourika Valley is a short drive into the Atlas Mountains from Marrakech. The road follows a river through Berber villages, terraced fields and small markets, and it ends at a village where you can walk to waterfalls. It is the easiest day trip, ideal in summer when the city is hot, and it suits families and travellers with little time. See the Ourika Valley day trip.

Ouzoud Waterfalls

Ouzoud is one of the tallest waterfalls in Africa, in a green gorge of the Middle Atlas. You walk down a path with views of the falls, take a small boat to the base and meet the Barbary macaques that live in the trees. It is a longer day, but the setting is dramatic and the pools at the bottom are a good place to cool off in the warm season. Read our full guide to the Ouzoud Waterfalls and see the Ouzoud day trip.

Ait Ben Haddou and Ouarzazate

Ait Ben Haddou is a UNESCO-listed ksar of red-earth towers on an old caravan route, used in many films. The road crosses the High Atlas by the Tizi n'Tichka pass, with wide views, and you can stop in Ouarzazate on the way. It is the most spectacular day trip, but also the longest, so start early and bring snacks. If you prefer a slower pace, a 2, 3 or 4-day trip towards the Sahara includes the ksar. Read our guide to Ait Ben Haddou and see the Ait Ben Haddou day trip.

Essaouira

Essaouira is a calm Atlantic port with a blue-and-white medina, a fortified seafront, a fishing harbour and a long beach. The sea breeze makes it a welcome change from Marrakech in summer. It is usually better with a night, but it works as a long day trip. Read our things to do in Essaouira and ask us for a private transfer.

Agafay desert

The Agafay desert is a stony landscape of hills and plains less than an hour from Marrakech, with the Atlas in the distance. You can ride a quad or a camel and stay for a sunset dinner. It is a half-day or evening plan rather than a full day. See Agafay desert from Marrakech and compare it with the Sahara in Agafay or the Sahara.

The Atlas Mountains

Villages, walks and cooler air are waiting in the High Atlas, within one to two hours from the city. It is a good choice for walkers, for summer days and for travellers who like Berber culture. Read our guide to an Atlas Mountains day trip from Marrakech.

How to choose your day trip

  • Short on time: choose the Ourika Valley or the Agafay desert.
  • Love waterfalls and nature: choose Ouzoud, with a boat ride and monkeys.
  • Love history and film locations: choose Ait Ben Haddou, with Ouarzazate.
  • Want the sea: choose Essaouira, ideally with a night.
  • Travelling with children: choose Ourika or the Agafay camel ride, as they are closer.
  • Hot summer day: choose the mountains or the coast.

For a detailed comparison of three of these, read Ourika, Ouzoud or Ait Ben Haddou.

Sample day plans

Ourika Valley: a relaxed day

  • Morning: leave Marrakech by 9:00 and drive up the valley with photo stops.
  • Midday: visit a Berber village and have tea with a local family, or walk by the river.
  • Lunch: eat a simple tagine at a riverside terrace.
  • Afternoon: take a short walk toward the waterfalls, if the weather is good.
  • Evening: return to Marrakech in the late afternoon.

Ouzoud Waterfalls: a full day

  • Early morning: leave Marrakech by 8:00, as the drive is long.
  • Late morning: walk down the path with views of the falls and meet the macaques.
  • Lunch: eat at a terrace overlooking the water.
  • Afternoon: take the boat ride to the base of the falls.
  • Evening: drive back and arrive in Marrakech at the end of the day.

Ait Ben Haddou: a long and rewarding day

  • Early morning: leave by 7:30 and cross the Tizi n'Tichka pass.
  • Late morning: visit the ksar with a guide and climb to the top for the view.
  • Lunch: eat in or near the village.
  • Afternoon: stop in Ouarzazate on the way back, if you like.
  • Evening: return to Marrakech late; bring a snack and water.

These are examples. We adjust the start time, the stops and the pace to your group.

Day trip or multi-day tour?

A day trip is perfect if you have a short time or want to see one place. But if you want the Sahara, the Dades Valley or Merzouga, a day trip is not enough, because the distances are too long. A 2-day tour reaches the desert, a 3-day tour allows a relaxed pace, and a 4-day tour adds more places. Compare the options in our guides to Merzouga or Zagora and excursions from Marrakech to the Sahara. If you want to start in Marrakech and finish in Fes, see the Marrakech to Fes desert tour.

Who each option suits

  • Day trip: travellers with a short stay, families with young children and people who prefer to sleep in the same hotel.
  • 2-day tour: people who want a taste of the desert without a long trip.
  • 3 to 4-day tour: travellers who want the full experience of the Sahara and the south, at a comfortable pace.

Which day trip for which traveller?

  • Families with children: the Ourika Valley or an Agafay camel ride, with short drives and easy walks.
  • Couples: Essaouira for a romantic evening by the sea, or the Agafay desert for a sunset dinner.
  • Photographers: Ait Ben Haddou in the morning light and Ouzoud for the falls and the green gorge.
  • Walkers: the Ourika Valley and the Atlas, with paths and villages.
  • Older travellers: shorter trips with fewer steps, such as Ourika or Agafay, and a private vehicle with stops.
  • History and film lovers: Ait Ben Haddou and Ouarzazate, with the film studios.

Tell us who travels, what you like and your comfort level, and we recommend the best trip. We can also adapt the pace for children, for older travellers and for people who need more breaks.

Best time for day trips from Marrakech

Spring and autumn are the best seasons, with warm days and clear light. In summer, the plains are very hot, so choose the mountains, the waterfalls or the coast. In winter, snow can fall in the Atlas, which is beautiful but can change the road, and the mornings are cold. Check the Marrakech weather before you choose.

Tips for day trips from Marrakech

  • Start early: the roads are quiet and the light is good in the morning.
  • Wear comfortable shoes: trails to waterfalls and ksars can be uneven.
  • Bring water and snacks: distances are long and some stops are remote.
  • Carry cash: for tips, small shops and local cafes.
  • Plan one trip a day: distances are long, and a rest day helps.

Best day trips from Marrakech: frequently asked questions

What are the best day trips from Marrakech?

The best day trips from Marrakech are the Ourika Valley, the Ouzoud Waterfalls, Ait Ben Haddou and the Ouarzazate region, Essaouira on the coast, the Agafay desert and the Atlas Mountains. Our private day trips cover Ourika, Ouzoud and Ait Ben Haddou, and each one returns to Marrakech the same day.

Which day trip from Marrakech is the closest?

The Ourika Valley and the Agafay desert are the closest, both about an hour from Marrakech. The Ouzoud Waterfalls, Essaouira and Ait Ben Haddou are further and make a longer day.

Which is better, Ourika or Ouzoud?

Ourika is closer and easier, with a river, Berber villages and waterfalls, and is good if you have a short day. Ouzoud has a much taller waterfall, a boat ride and monkeys, and needs a longer drive. Choose Ourika for ease and Ouzoud for the spectacle.

Can I visit Ait Ben Haddou in a day from Marrakech?

Yes, but it is a long day. The road crosses the High Atlas by the Tizi n'Tichka pass, and you can combine the ksar with Ouarzazate. If you prefer a slower pace, choose a 2 or 3-day tour towards the Sahara.

Are the day trips private?

Yes. Our day trips are private, with transport from Marrakech and a local guide on request, and you return to Marrakech the same day.

How much does a day trip from Marrakech cost?

The price depends on the trip, the date and the number of people, so we quote on request. Send us your dates and we reply with a free quote with no obligation.

What is the best time of year for day trips from Marrakech?

Spring and autumn are the most comfortable. Summer is very hot in the plains, so the mountains are a good choice, and winter can bring snow in the Atlas and cold mornings. Check the weather before you go.

Can I do two day trips in one day?

It is not a good idea, because the distances are long. If you have little time, combine the Agafay desert with a visit to the medina, or choose Ourika, which is close, and keep the evening free.

What should I bring on a day trip?

Bring comfortable shoes, water, sun protection, a light jacket and some cash for tips and small purchases. For the waterfalls, wear shoes with grip, and for the mountains, bring a warm layer.

Do the day trips include lunch?

Lunch and entrance fees depend on the trip and are confirmed in your free quote. Tell us your preferences and we adapt the plan.

How do I book a day trip from Marrakech?

Send us your dates and number of travellers with the contact form, by WhatsApp or by email. We reply with a free, no-obligation quote and, when you are happy with it, we confirm the booking.
