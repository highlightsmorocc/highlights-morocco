The short answer

The best time to visit Morocco is spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November), when days are warm and not extreme in the cities, the mountains and the Sahara. Summer is very hot in Marrakech and the desert, so it suits the Atlantic coast and the high mountains better. Winter has pleasant days in the south, cold desert nights and snow in the High Atlas. The best month depends on the regions you want to visit.

Morocco is a country of contrasts: the coast, the cities, the mountains and the desert have different climates. This guide helps you choose the right dates for your trip. If you want a quick recommendation, message us on WhatsApp with your dates.

Ramadan and holidays

Ramadan follows the lunar calendar, so it starts about 11 days earlier each year. Check the exact dates for the year you travel. During Ramadan, tourism continues, many visitors enjoy the atmosphere around sunset, and most tourist services keep working, but daytime opening hours and restaurants can change, and the pace is slower.

  • Respect fasting in public by avoiding eating and drinking openly in small towns.
  • Plan long drives and visits in the morning.
  • Expect a festive atmosphere after sunset, when the fast is broken.
  • Christmas, New Year and Eid are busy periods, so book earlier.

How to choose your dates

  • If you want the Sahara: choose March to May or October to November.
  • If you want cities and culture: spring and autumn are the most comfortable.
  • If you want the mountains: late spring to early autumn for walking, winter for snow.
  • If you want the coast: summer is the best season.
  • If you want fewer crowds: consider January, February or the start and the end of the busy seasons.

Best time to visit Morocco: frequently asked questions

What is the best time to visit Morocco?

Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) are usually the best seasons for a trip that combines cities, mountains and the Sahara, because days are warm and not extreme. Summer is very hot inland and in the desert, and winter is cold at night but pleasant by day in the south.

What is the best month to visit Morocco?

April and October are popular choices because the weather is pleasant in most regions. May and November are also good, with fewer very hot days in the south.

What is the best time to visit the Sahara in Morocco?

Spring and autumn, when the days are warm and the nights are comfortable. Summer is very hot in Merzouga, and winter nights in the desert can be cold, so bring warm clothes.

What is the best time to visit Marrakech?

March to May and September to November are the most comfortable. Summer afternoons in Marrakech are very hot, so plan sightseeing early in the morning and late in the afternoon.

Is summer a bad time to visit Morocco?

Not necessarily, but it is very hot in Marrakech, in the interior and in the desert. Summer suits the Atlantic coast, such as Essaouira, and the high mountains better than the Sahara. If you travel in summer, start early and rest in the middle of the day.

Is winter a good time to visit Morocco?

Yes, for city breaks and winter sun in the south, with pleasant days and cold nights in the desert. There can be snow in the High Atlas, and some mountain roads may be affected, so ask your tour operator about conditions.

When is Ramadan in Morocco and should I avoid it?

Ramadan follows the lunar calendar, so it moves about 11 days earlier each year, and you should check the dates for the year you travel. Tourism continues during Ramadan, and some visitors enjoy the atmosphere, but daytime opening hours and restaurant options can change, and the pace is slower.

When are the cheapest months to travel to Morocco?

Prices and demand are usually highest in spring, autumn and the Christmas and New Year period. Quieter months, such as January and February or the summer, can be better value, but the weather is colder or hotter.

How far in advance should I book a Morocco tour?

As early as you can for spring and autumn, when drivers, camps and riads fill up. For a short trip, a few weeks may be enough, and for long or tailor-made trips, a few months is better.
