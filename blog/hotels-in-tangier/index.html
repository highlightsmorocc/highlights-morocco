Where to stay in Tangier

The best areas and types of hotels in Tangier, from riads in the medina to seafront hotels, with tips for choosing and booking.

The short answer

The best place to stay in Tangier depends on your trip. Choose a riad in the medina or the Kasbah for history and atmosphere, the modern city centre for comfort, restaurants and parking, or the seafront near the corniche for sea views. Historic hotels such as El Minzah and the Hotel Continental add character. One night is enough in transit, and two nights let you see Cap Spartel and the Caves of Hercules without rushing.

Tangier is a port city with a mix of old and new, so the area where you sleep changes your trip. A riad in the medina puts you among whitewashed lanes and mosques. A hotel in the new city is close to cafes, shops and the train station. A seafront hotel gives you morning views of the strait. This guide explains the best areas, the types of places to stay, how to choose and how to book. When you want help, message us on WhatsApp and we plan it for you.

The areas of Tangier in detail

The medina and the Kasbah

This is the most atmospheric place to stay. You wake up to the sound of the old town, walk to the Petit Socco, the Kasbah Museum and the viewpoints in a few minutes, and eat breakfast on a roof terrace with the strait in front of you. The trade-off is access: taxis stop at the gates, lanes are steep and some riads have stairs without a lift. Pack light, or ask the riad to send a porter to meet you.

The city centre

The modern centre has wide streets, cafes, restaurants, banks and shops, and it is easy to reach the train station and the bus station. It is a practical choice if you arrive late, travel with a lot of luggage or like to have everything nearby. You can walk or take a short taxi to the medina.

The seafront and the corniche

The long promenade along the bay has hotels, beach cafes and views of the sea. It is lively in summer evenings, with families walking and restaurants serving fish. If you want to swim, take morning walks and see the sunset from your balcony, this is a good area. Ask for a room on a high floor or away from the road if you want less noise.

Marshan and the western hill

Marshan is a quieter residential area on the hill above the sea, near Cafe Hafa and the road to Cap Spartel. It has some guesthouses and villas with garden views. It suits travellers who want calm and a sea view, as long as they accept a short taxi ride to the centre.

Types of hotels and places to stay in Tangier

Riads and guesthouses

Riads are traditional houses with a central courtyard. In Tangier many are in the medina and the Kasbah, some with a roof terrace that looks over the strait. They are small and personal, with a breakfast served in the courtyard, and they suit couples and travellers who like local character. Some are on narrow lanes, so you may need help with your luggage.

Boutique hotels

Boutique hotels mix design, comfort and a small number of rooms. They are a good choice if you want style and quiet without a large resort. They often have a restaurant, a terrace or a small pool.

Seafront and city hotels

Larger hotels in the new city and along the seafront offer lifts, air conditioning, parking and a restaurant, and some have pools or sea views. They suit families, groups and business travellers who want easy access and services.

Budget hotels and hostels

Tangier has simple hotels and hostels in and around the medina and the centre for travellers on a tight budget. Check recent reviews, the location and the heating and air conditioning, and look for a clean, safe place with a good reception.

Apartments and holiday rentals

For longer stays, an apartment gives you a kitchen and more space. Make sure the host explains the address, the check-in and any rules clearly, especially in the medina where streets can be hard to find.

Where to stay in Tangier for each traveller

  • Couples: a riad in the medina or the Kasbah, with a terrace and a quiet courtyard.
  • Families: a hotel in the city centre or on the seafront, with parking, a lift and air conditioning.
  • First-time visitors: the edge of the medina, so you can walk to the sights and still reach restaurants and taxis easily.
  • Solo travellers: a guesthouse or hostel in a central, busy street with good reviews.
  • Business travellers: a hotel in the city centre near the station and the main roads.
  • Travellers with a ferry or an early train: a hotel near the port or the station, so you can leave without a long transfer.

Historic hotels in Tangier

Tangier has a long hotel tradition that goes back to the time when it was an international city and a stop for writers, artists and diplomats. Two names often come up. El Minzah is a famous hotel in the city centre with an Andalusian-style design and a long guest history. The Hotel Continental is an old hotel overlooking the port, known for its views and its atmosphere. Even if you do not stay, a drink or a tea in a historic hotel is a pleasant way to feel the past of the city. Always check the current facilities, as older buildings vary in standard.

How to choose a hotel in Tangier

  • Location: decide whether you prefer the old town, the centre or the seafront.
  • Access: ask if a car or taxi can reach the door, or if you must walk from a gate.
  • Climate: winters are cool and damp, so ask about heating; summers can be warm, so check for air conditioning.
  • Noise: light sleepers should ask for a room away from the street and the call to prayer.
  • Breakfast: check what is included and the time it starts.
  • Reviews: read recent comments about cleanliness, service and safety.
  • Cancellation: choose flexible terms if your plans can change.

What affects the price of a hotel in Tangier

Prices change a lot by season, area and type of place, so we do not quote a fixed number here. The main factors are the time of year, with summer, Easter and Christmas the most expensive, the location, with the medina and the seafront in demand, the size and style of the room, and whether breakfast and transfers are included. Booking early and travelling in spring or autumn usually gives you more choice. If you tell us your budget, we propose the best place for it.

  • Room type: a courtyard room or a sea-view room usually costs more than a standard room.
  • Season: the high season fills quickly, so book early.
  • Length of stay: some places offer better terms for two or more nights.
  • Inclusions: check breakfast, airport or port transfers and parking.

Hotels near the port, station and airport

Tangier has a ferry port in the city, a high-speed train station, a bus station and an airport about 15 km away. If you arrive by ferry from Spain, a hotel in the medina or the city centre is only a short ride from the port. If you arrive at Tanger Med, about 40 km east, you need a transfer. If you take an early flight or ferry, a hotel close to the airport, the port or the station saves time. Tell us your arrival and we arrange a driver.

When to book hotels in Tangier

Summer is busy because many Moroccans living abroad and many Europeans visit, and the hotels fill up. Easter, Christmas and long weekends are also busy. Spring and autumn are calmer and pleasant, with good weather, and winter is quiet. In all seasons the best riads and boutique hotels are small, so book early. Read our guide to the best time to visit Morocco for the whole country.

How many nights to stay in Tangier

One night works if you are passing through on the way to Chefchaouen or Fes. Two nights are better, with one day for the medina, the Kasbah and Cafe Hafa, and another for Cap Spartel, the Caves of Hercules and the beach. If you plan day trips, use Tangier as a base: Asilah is close, Tetouan is a short drive and Chefchaouen is about two hours away. Read our guide on Tangier to Chefchaouen for the route.

Hotels in Tangier: frequently asked questions

Where is the best area to stay in Tangier?

For atmosphere, stay in the medina or the Kasbah in a riad. For comfort, restaurants and easy parking, stay in the modern city centre. For sea views, choose the seafront near the corniche. First-time visitors usually enjoy the medina or the city centre the most.

Is it better to stay in the medina or the new town in Tangier?

The medina gives you history, narrow lanes and traditional riads, but luggage and parking can be harder. The new town is easier for cars, taxis and restaurants. If you want both, choose a hotel on the edge of the medina.

Are there riads in Tangier?

Yes. Tangier has riads and small guesthouses in the medina and the Kasbah, many in restored historic houses with a courtyard, a terrace and views over the strait. They are usually small, so book early in busy periods.

How many nights should I stay in Tangier?

One night is enough to see the medina and Cap Spartel if you are passing through. Two nights give you time for the Caves of Hercules, the beach and a slower pace, and make a good base for day trips to Asilah, Tetouan and Chefchaouen.

Which hotels in Tangier are historic?

Well-known historic names include El Minzah and the Hotel Continental, both tied to the story of international Tangier. Many travellers stay in them or visit for a drink to see the atmosphere. Check each hotel for current facilities and standards before you book.

Where should I stay if I arrive by ferry?

If you arrive at the port in Tangier, a hotel in the medina or the city centre is close. If your ferry arrives at Tanger Med, which is about 40 km from the city, allow time for the transfer, or ask us to arrange a driver.

Which area is best for families?

Families often prefer the modern city centre or a hotel near the seafront, where it is easier to park, eat out and walk on the corniche. A hotel with air conditioning and a lift is helpful with young children.

Is Tangier safe to stay in?

Tangier is generally safe for visitors. Choose a well-located hotel, keep your valuables in the safe and use licensed taxis at night. See our guide to things to do in Tangier for more safety tips.

When is the best time to book hotels in Tangier?

Book early for summer, Easter, Christmas and the holidays when many Moroccans and Europeans travel. In the low season you can often book closer to your date, but good riads fill up, so reserve in advance.

Do hotels in Tangier include breakfast?

Many riads and hotels include a Moroccan breakfast with bread, olive oil, jam, eggs and mint tea. Always check what is included, especially if you leave early for a tour.
