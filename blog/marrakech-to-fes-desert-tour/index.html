The short answer

The best way to go from Marrakech to Fes with the Sahara is our 3-day private desert tour via Merzouga. You cross the High Atlas, visit Ait Ben Haddou and the Dades Valley, ride a camel at sunset, sleep in a desert camp and finish in Fes through the Middle Atlas. It starts from 249 euros per person, with a private driver and a local guide who speaks English or Spanish. The direct road takes 7 to 8 hours, but the tour is a journey, not just a transfer.

Marrakech and Fes are the two great imperial cities of Morocco, and many travellers want to link them. You can take a train, a bus or a car and arrive in a day, or you can turn the journey into a three-day adventure through the desert. This guide compares the options, shows the route day by day, explains what is included and how the price works, and tells you how to book. Why go from Marrakech to Fes via the Sahara?

  • One route, no backtracking: you cross the country and finish in Fes, instead of returning to Marrakech.
  • The best landscapes: the High Atlas, the red ksar of Ait Ben Haddou, the Dades Valley and the dunes of Erg Chebbi.
  • A night in the desert: a camel ride at sunset, dinner at camp and a sunrise over the dunes.
  • A local guide: someone who explains what you see and helps with every stop.
  • Comfort and flexibility: a private driver, a vehicle for your group and stops where you like.

For more about the destination, read our guides to Merzouga or Zagora and Ait Ben Haddou, and the checklist before booking a Sahara tour.

The 3-day Marrakech to Fes route

The route is Marrakech, Ait Ben Haddou, the Dades Valley, Merzouga and the Middle Atlas to Fes. It crosses the High Atlas by the Tizi n'Tichka pass, passes Ouarzazate, follows the Dades and Todra valleys to the south-east, reaches the Erg Chebbi dunes at Merzouga and returns north through Midelt and the cedar forests of the Middle Atlas. The tour has two long driving days and one shorter day, with stops for photos and lunch.

Day by day itinerary

Day 1: Marrakech to the Dades Valley

Leave Marrakech in the morning with your private driver and local guide. Cross the High Atlas by the Tizi n'Tichka pass, visit the UNESCO-listed ksar of Ait Ben Haddou and stop in Ouarzazate. Continue to the Dades Valley and spend the night there.

Day 2: Todra Gorges to Merzouga

Visit the Todra Gorges and drive across the desert plains of the south-east to Merzouga. Ride a camel at sunset into the Erg Chebbi dunes, enjoy dinner and sleep in a desert camp under the stars.

Day 3: Merzouga to Fes

Watch the sunrise over the dunes and have breakfast at camp. Drive north through the Middle Atlas, with a lunch stop in the mountains, near the cedar forest area. Arrive in Fes in the evening, at the point we agree with you. This is the longest day, about 460 km.

What you see on the way

  • Tizi n'Tichka pass: the High Atlas road, with wide views of the mountains and small villages.
  • Ait Ben Haddou: a UNESCO-listed ksar of red-earth towers on an old caravan route, used in many films.
  • Ouarzazate: a stop at the gateway of the desert and the film studios.
  • Dades Valley: rock formations, palm groves and a quiet night on the road.
  • Todra Gorges: tall cliffs and a green river valley, good for a short walk.
  • Erg Chebbi, Merzouga: the highest dunes of Morocco, a camel ride and a night in camp.
  • Middle Atlas: cedar forests, mountain towns and a change of scenery on the way to Fes.

Our guide explains each stop and gives you time for photos, so you do not rush. For more on the ksar, read our guide to Ait Ben Haddou.

The desert camp in Merzouga

On day two you ride a camel at sunset from the edge of the dunes to a camp in the Erg Chebbi. The camp has tents with beds, a dining tent and a place to sit around the fire, where dinner is served and local musicians sometimes play. At night the sky is very clear, and in the morning you can climb a dune to watch the sunrise. Facilities depend on the type of camp, from a simple bivouac to a more comfortable one, and we confirm what you prefer in the quote. Bring a warm layer, because the desert gets cold at night, and a head torch can help.

Is this tour for you?

  • Yes, if: you want to see the Atlas, the Dades Valley and the Sahara, and finish in Fes without going back.
  • Yes, if: you travel as a couple, with friends or as a family and want a private vehicle and guide.
  • Maybe not, if: you dislike long drives. Day one and day three are long, so choose 4 days for a slower pace.
  • Maybe not, if: you only want to travel between the cities in one day. A train or a direct transfer is faster.

If you want more time in each place, we can add a night in the Dades Valley, in Merzouga or in the Middle Atlas, and we can start or end the tour in other cities. Tell us what you need.

Price and how it is set

The final price depends on your dates, the number of travellers and the options you choose, such as the type of desert camp. The more people share the vehicle, the lower the price per person. Fes to Marrakech: the same tour in reverse

You can do the same journey in the opposite direction, from Fes to Marrakech through the Middle Atlas, Merzouga, the Dades Valley and the High Atlas. This works well if you arrive in Morocco in the north, for example in Tangier or Fes, and leave from Marrakech. See our tours from Fes and our guide to things to do in Fes.

What to pack for the tour

  • Layers: mornings and desert nights are cold, even after a hot day.
  • A warm jacket: especially from autumn to spring.
  • Sun protection: a hat, sunglasses and sun cream.
  • A scarf: to protect your face from sun and sand.
  • Closed shoes: for the dunes and the gorges.
  • A small bag: pack a night bag for the camp and leave the large one in the vehicle.
  • Cash: for tips, drinks and small purchases.

For more advice, read our Morocco travel tips.

Best time to do the Marrakech to Fes desert tour

Spring and autumn are the best seasons, with warm days and comfortable nights. Summer is very hot in the south, so we plan early starts and long breaks. Winter has clear days, cold desert nights and sometimes snow in the High Atlas, which can change the route. Marrakech to Fes desert tour: frequently asked questions

How much is a 3-day Marrakech to Fes desert tour?

Our private 3-day Marrakech to Fes desert tour starts from 249 euros per person. The final price depends on your dates and group size, and we send a free quote with no obligation.

How long is the desert tour from Marrakech to Fes?

The tour takes 3 days and 2 nights: Marrakech to the Dades Valley on day one, Merzouga and a night in a desert camp on day two, and Merzouga to Fes on day three. Day three is about 460 km, so the days are long.

Is the Marrakech to Fes desert tour private?

Yes. It is a private tour with your own driver and a local guide for your group only. You choose the stops, the pace and the pick-up point.

Does the tour include a night in the Sahara?

Yes. It includes one night in a desert camp near the Erg Chebbi dunes in Merzouga, with a camel ride at sunset, dinner and breakfast at camp. The other night is in the Dades Valley.

What languages does the guide speak?

Our guides speak English and Spanish. Tell us your language when you ask for a quote.

How far is Marrakech from Fes?

The direct road between Marrakech and Fes is roughly 530 km and takes about 7 to 8 hours of driving. The Sahara route is longer, because it goes south to Merzouga, and it is spread over three days.

Can I go from Marrakech to Fes by train or bus?

Yes. Trains and buses connect the two cities, usually with a change, and the journey takes most of a day. It is cheaper, but you do not see the Atlas, the Dades Valley or the desert. We can also arrange a private transfer.

Can I do the tour from Fes to Marrakech?

Yes. The tour works in both directions. The Fes to Marrakech version finishes in Marrakech after the Sahara, the Dades Valley and the High Atlas.

Is the Marrakech to Fes desert tour worth it?

Yes, if you want to cross Morocco without going back to the start. You see the High Atlas, Ait Ben Haddou, the Dades Valley, the dunes of Merzouga and the Middle Atlas, and you finish in Fes. The days are long, so choose 4 days if you prefer a slower pace.

What is the best time of year for this tour?

Spring and autumn are the most comfortable seasons. Summer is very hot in the south and winter nights in the desert are cold, so bring warm layers.

How do I book the Marrakech to Fes desert tour?

Send us your dates and number of travellers with the contact form, by WhatsApp or by email. We reply with a free, no-obligation quote and, when you are happy with it, we confirm the booking.
