What is a Morocco package tour?

A package tour combines the main parts of a trip into one booking. Instead of arranging a driver, a guide, hotels, a camel ride and a desert camp separately, you buy a route in which all of these are included, usually with fixed days and a clear itinerary. The advantage is simplicity: one team organises the trip, you know what you pay and you do not have to manage long distances. In Morocco, where the main sights are far apart and the roads are long, a private package with a driver and a guide is the most practical way to travel.

Package tour or do it yourself?

#OptionAdvantagesLimits
1Private packageOne team, one price, a driver and a guide, no stressLess spontaneity than a completely free trip
2Group packageThe lowest price per person, and you meet other travellersA set programme and a shared vehicle
3Rental carFull freedom and low daily cost for a groupMountain roads, long distances, parking and no local guide
4Trains and busesCheap between big citiesThey do not reach the desert and the mountains easily

Morocco has a good train line between the big cities, but the Sahara, the Atlas and the kasbahs are best reached by road. A private package with a driver removes the stress of long mountain drives and lets you enjoy the views, and a guide adds the history and the context. If you like to plan everything yourself, you can combine a package for the Sahara with your own plans for the cities.

A typical day on a package tour

On a classic 3-day desert package, the first day starts in the morning in Marrakech, with a drive over the High Atlas by the Tizi n'Tichka pass, a stop at Ait Ben Haddou, lunch in Ouarzazate and a night in the Dades Valley. On the second day you visit the Todra Gorges, drive across the plains to Merzouga, ride a camel at sunset and sleep in a desert camp. On the third day you watch the sunrise over the dunes and drive back. The days are long, the stops are frequent and your guide explains what you see, so the time in the vehicle is part of the experience. See the full 3-day itinerary.

How to compare package offers fairly

  • Compare the same things: days, nights, meals, vehicle and guide; a cheaper offer may include less.
  • Ask about the vehicle: a private car or van, not a shared bus, unless you chose a group tour.
  • Check the accommodation: the names or the type of riads, hotels and camps.
  • Check the camp: the type of tent, the bathrooms and the meals.
  • Driving time: ask how many hours you drive each day.
  • Read the exclusions: entrance fees, lunches, drinks and tips.
  • Check the team: ask who answers on WhatsApp and who helps during the trip.

Price of a Morocco package and what changes it

Our 3-day desert tours from Marrakech to Merzouga and from Marrakech to Fes start from 249 euros per person, and the 4-day Merzouga tour starts from 319 euros per person. The final price depends on your dates, the number of travellers, the type of camp and rooms and the activities you add. A larger group shares the cost of the vehicle and the guide, so the price per person falls. For longer routes, the price is on request, with a free quote and no obligation, so you know exactly what is included before you book.

  • Group size: the more people share the vehicle, the lower the price per person.
  • Season: the high season and the holidays can change prices and availability.
  • Comfort level: standard or higher-level riads, hotels and camps.
  • Length: more days mean more nights and more driving.
  • Extras: activities, extra nights and private transfers.

Red flags when you compare Morocco tours

  • A price that looks too low: check what is missing, such as the vehicle, the camp or the guide.
  • No details about the camp or the hotels: ask for the type and the inclusions.
  • No clear list of what is included: a good offer shows it in writing.
  • Pressure to pay quickly: you should have time to read and ask.
  • No contact during the trip: you need a number to call if something changes.
  • Hidden extras: ask about fees, tips and optional activities in advance.

Best time for a Morocco package tour

Spring and autumn are the most comfortable seasons for most routes. Summer is very hot in the south and in the desert, so the nights in the camp are the best part, and the days need early starts. Winter has mild days, cold desert nights and sometimes snow in the High Atlas. Read our best time to visit Morocco guide to choose.

Morocco package tours guide: frequently asked questions

What is a Morocco package tour?

A Morocco package tour is a trip sold as one plan: the route, the transport, the guide, the accommodation and the main activities are organised for you and priced together, so you do not need to book each part. Ours are private, with a driver and a local guide.

What is included in a Morocco package tour?

Usually the private transport with a driver, a local guide, accommodation on the route, and the main activities such as a camel ride and a night in a desert camp. Lunches, drinks, tips and entrance fees are often not included. We confirm every detail in your free quote.

How much does a Morocco package tour cost?

Our 3-day desert tours from Marrakech to Merzouga and from Marrakech to Fes start from 249 euros per person, and the 4-day Merzouga tour from 319 euros per person. The final price depends on your dates and group size, and we send a free quote with no obligation.

Are your Morocco packages private?

Yes. All our tours are private, with your own vehicle, driver and guide. We also offer shared and small-group options on request.

How many days do I need for a Morocco package tour?

Three days is the classic minimum to reach the Sahara. Four to five days give a more relaxed pace, eight to ten days add Fes, Chefchaouen and the coast, and fourteen days cover the main regions without rushing.

Can I customise a package?

Yes. Every package is flexible. We change the route, the nights, the stays, the activities and the start and end city, and we build a tailor-made trip if you prefer.

Is a package tour better than booking each part myself?

A package saves time, avoids long drives by public transport and puts one team in charge of the whole trip. Booking each part gives more control, but it takes more planning and more risk of gaps. For the Sahara, a package with a private driver is the easiest option.

What should I ask before I book a package?

Ask what is included and what is not, the group size, the vehicle, the type of camp and rooms, the language of the guide, how many hours you drive each day and how to reach the team during the trip.

What is the best time of year for a Morocco tour package?

Spring and autumn are the most comfortable seasons. Summer is very hot in the south and winter nights in the desert are cold, so choose the dates and the clothes accordingly.

Do you offer packages starting in other cities?

Yes. We offer tours from Marrakech, Fes, Casablanca, Rabat, Tangier and Errachidia, and one-way routes that end in a different city.

How do I book a Morocco package tour?

Send us your dates and number of travellers with the contact form, by WhatsApp or by email. We reply with a free, no-obligation quote and, when you are happy with it, we confirm the booking.
