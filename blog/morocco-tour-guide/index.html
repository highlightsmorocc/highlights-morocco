Why hire a private guide

  • Orientation: the medinas of Marrakech and Fes are narrow and confusing, and a guide gets you through them calmly.
  • Context: a guide explains the history, the crafts and the customs behind what you see.
  • Time: a guide skips the queues and the detours, so you see more in less time.
  • Safety: in the desert and the mountains a local guide knows the tracks, the weather and the risks.
  • Language: a guide who speaks your language makes every conversation easier.

What a guide does in each place

In Marrakech

Your guide takes you through the medina, the souks, the palaces and the gardens at a pace you choose, and explains the history of the city. Read things to do in Marrakech and our guide to the Jemaa el-Fnaa square.

In Fes

The medina of Fes is one of the largest car-free areas in the world, and a local guide is the best way to see the tanneries, the madrasas and the workshops. See things to do in Fes.

In the Sahara

Your guide leads the camel caravan, chooses the camp and the route, and introduces you to the families who live in the desert. Read camel riding in Morocco and our Sahara desert tours guide.

Types of guide in Morocco

  • City guide: a local who takes you through one city on foot, in the medina, the souks and the main sights.
  • Driver-guide: a person who drives and guides at the same time on a day trip or a short route.
  • Desert guide: a guide who leads the camel caravan and looks after the camp and the route in the Sahara.
  • Tour guide for a whole route: a guide who stays with your group for several days and several cities.

Each type suits a different trip, and we recommend the right one when you tell us your plans.

Sample days with a private guide

  • A day in Marrakech: a morning in the medina and the souks, lunch in a local place, the palaces in the afternoon and the Jemaa el-Fnaa square at dusk.
  • A day in Fes: the tanneries, the madrasas and the workshops in the morning, a long lunch and a viewpoint over the old city.
  • A day trip: a drive to Ouzoud or Ourika with a guide who knows the best paths and a good place to eat.

Every plan can be changed on the day, and your guide adapts the pace to the group.

What to check before you hire

  • Licence: ask whether the guide is licensed and how long they have worked in the area.
  • Languages: confirm that the guide speaks your language well.
  • A written quote: the hours, the places, the language and the price are in writing.
  • What is included: transport, entrances, meals and tips are listed.
  • A real contact: a WhatsApp number and an email that answer quickly.
  • Clear limits: the team tells you honestly what the guide can and cannot do.

Tips for the day with your guide

  • Tell your guide what you like: crafts, food, history or photography change the route.
  • Wear comfortable shoes: medina streets are uneven and you walk a lot.
  • Carry water and sun protection: the sun is strong, even in winter.
  • Agree on the meeting point: medina streets are narrow, so choose a clear place.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Hiring on the street at the last minute: a guide booked in advance gives you a clear price and a clear plan.

  • Skipping the guide in the desert: the dunes are not a place to go alone.

Morocco tour guide: frequently asked questions

How do I hire a tour guide in Morocco?

Send your dates, the city or route and the number of travellers to a local team by WhatsApp, email or the contact form. A good agency replies with a written quote that states the languages, the hours and what is included.

How much does a private guide in Morocco cost?

The price of a guide on its own depends on the city, the hours and the language, so we quote it on request, free and with no obligation. On our private tours, the local guide is included: desert tours start from 249 euros per person for 3 days, 349 euros for 4 days and 489 euros for 5 days.

Do your guides speak English and Spanish?

Our guides speak English or Spanish. Tell us your language when you ask for a quote, and we match you with the right guide.

Do I need a guide in the Marrakech or Fes medina?

A guide is very helpful in the medinas, because the streets are narrow and hard to navigate. A local guide shows you the best places, explains the history and helps you avoid pressure at the stalls.

Do I need a guide in the Sahara?

Yes, for your safety. The dunes have no marked tracks and change with the wind, and your guide leads the camels, picks the camp and knows the families of the desert.

How can I check that a guide is reliable?

Ask whether the guide is licensed, which languages they speak, how long they have worked in the area and what the quote includes. A reliable agency answers these questions clearly and in writing.

Is the guide included in your tours?

Yes. Every private tour has a driver and a local guide for your group only, and the guide stays with you for the whole route.

Can I book a guide for one day only?

Yes. A guide for a day in a medina or a day trip can be arranged on request. Tell us the date, the place and the hours.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept PayPal, Visa, Mastercard, American Express and cash. We explain the payment steps in your quote.
