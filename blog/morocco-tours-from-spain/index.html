Morocco Tours from Spain

The short answer

The easiest way to tour Morocco from Spain is to take the ferry from Tarifa to Tangier, which takes about one hour, and join a private tour with a driver and a local guide who meets you in Tangier. A day trip to Tangier is possible but rushed, so we recommend at least 5 days. Our private tours from Tangier start from 489 euros per person for 5 days to Marrakech and from 649 euros for 10 days, with a free quote.

Morocco is one short ferry away from southern Spain, and many travellers on the Costa del Sol, in Seville, Cadiz or the Algarve add it to their holiday. The crossing is easy, but the distances inside Morocco are long. This guide explains the ferries, what a day trip really gives you, the private routes that start in Tangier, the documents you need and how to book. When you are ready, message us on WhatsApp for a free quote.

Ferries from Spain to Morocco

#CrossingTimePortBest for
1Tarifa to Tangier VilleAbout 1 hourIn the city of TangierFoot passengers and most tours
2Algeciras to Tanger MedAbout 1 to 1.5 hoursA commercial port, some 40 to 50 km from TangierTravellers with a vehicle

For most travellers, Tarifa to Tangier is the best crossing, because the port is in the city and the trip is short. The Algeciras to Tanger Med crossing is used mainly by cars and trucks, and its port is far from the city. Times, prices and schedules change with the operator and the weather, so check them before you travel.

Can you visit Morocco in one day from Spain?

Yes, but only Tangier is realistic. You cross in the morning, walk the medina and the kasbah, see Cap Spartel and return in the evening, with time lost at the port and the border. Chefchaouen is about 120 km each way from Tangier, which is too far for a rushed day. If you have a holiday week, a multi-day tour shows the real Morocco. See things to do in Tangier and Tangier to Chefchaouen.

The 5-day route from Tangier, day by day

  • Day 1: Tangier to Chefchaouen via Tetouan, about 120 km, and an evening walk in the blue town.
  • Day 2: Chefchaouen to Fes via Volubilis and Meknes, about 300 km with stops.
  • Day 3: Fes to Merzouga, about 460 km, with a camel ride at sunset and a night in a desert camp.
  • Day 4: Merzouga to the Dades Valley, with the Todra Gorges, about 300 km.
  • Day 5: the Dades Valley to Marrakech across the High Atlas, about 330 km. The tour ends there, and we can take you to your hotel or the airport.

Who is a tour from Spain for?

  • Holidaymakers in Andalusia: who want a few days of Morocco without long flights.
  • Residents in southern Spain: with a long weekend or a bank holiday to use.
  • Travellers from Portugal and Europe: who are already in Spain and want to cross the Strait.

In every case, the key is a route that matches your days. Five days are the minimum to see the north, Fes and the Sahara.

Three ways to use the ferry

  • Ferry in, flight out: cross to Tangier, take the 5 or 10-day tour south and fly home from Marrakech.
  • Flight in, ferry out: fly to Marrakech, take the route north and finish in Tangier for the ferry to Tarifa.
  • Ferry both ways: the 14-day loop starts and ends in Tangier, so you do not need a flight.

Check the flights and the ferry times before you fix the dates, and tell us when you book so that we plan the pick-up.

Getting to the port in Spain

Tarifa and Algeciras are easy to reach by bus or car from Malaga, Seville, Cadiz and Granada. In summer and at Easter the ferries fill up, so buy your ticket in advance, arrive early and keep your passport at hand.

Can I take my own car or a rental car?

Many rental companies do not allow their cars to enter Morocco, and taking your own car needs extra paperwork and insurance. The simple option is to leave the car in Spain and travel as a foot passenger. In Morocco, your private driver does all the driving, so you can enjoy the road and the stops.

Passport, money and phone

  • Passport: carry a valid passport. Entry formalities are done at the port or on board. Check your situation in our Morocco visa guide.
  • Money: Morocco uses the dirham. Take some small notes for tea and tips, and read about currency and tipping.
  • Phone: ask your operator about roaming in Morocco, and download offline maps.
  • Insurance: check that your travel insurance covers Morocco.

Tips for travelling to Morocco from Spain

  • A buffer day: wind can delay the ferries, so do not book a flight on the day you return.
  • Travel light: a small bag is easier on the ferry and in the riads.
  • Respect local customs: dress modestly in the medinas and ask before you photograph people.
  • Use your Spanish: our guides speak Spanish and English, which makes the trip easier.
  • Try the local food: tagine, couscous and mint tea.

A night in Tangier before the tour

If your ferry arrives late, spend a night in Tangier and start the tour the next morning. The medina, the kasbah with views over the Strait of Gibraltar, Cap Spartel and the Caves of Hercules are good for a first walk. See hotels in Tangier.

What is included and what it costs

Our private tours include a private driver, a local guide who speaks English or Spanish, accommodation as described in each route and, on desert tours, a camp night with dinner and breakfast. Ferry tickets, flights, lunches, drinks, entrance fees and tips are normally not included. The final price depends on the dates and the group, and the quote is free. See the cost of a Morocco tour.

The best time to go from Spain

Spring and autumn are the best seasons for a full route. In July and August the interior and the south are very hot, and the ferries are busy. Winter is mild on the coast but cold at night in the mountains and the desert. See the best time to visit Morocco.

Morocco tours from Spain: frequently asked questions

Can I do a day trip to Morocco from Spain?

Yes, to Tangier by ferry from Tarifa. It is rushed, with time lost at the port and the border, so a tour of 5 days or more shows far more of Morocco.

How long is the ferry from Spain to Morocco?

About one hour from Tarifa to Tangier. The crossing from Algeciras to Tanger Med takes longer, and its port is outside the city.

How much does a Morocco tour from Spain cost?

Our private tours from Tangier start from 489 euros per person for 5 days and 649 euros for 10 days. Ferry tickets and flights are not included, and the quote is free.

Will someone meet me in Tangier?

Yes. Your driver and local guide meet you in Tangier, at the point we agree with you, at the time that suits your ferry.

Can I end the tour in Spain?

Yes. The 5-day route from Marrakech to Tangier and the 14-day loop end in Tangier, so you can take the ferry back to Tarifa.

Do the guides speak Spanish?

Yes. Our local guides speak Spanish and English.

What is the best time to visit Morocco?

Spring, from March to May, and autumn, from September to November, are usually the most comfortable seasons. Summer is very hot in the south and inland, and winter nights can be cold.

Can I go with a private driver and a local guide?

Yes. All our tours are private, with your own driver and a local guide who speaks English or Spanish. We quote private trips on request, free and with no obligation.

How do I book a trip to Morocco?

Send us your dates and the number of travellers with the contact form, by WhatsApp or by email. We reply with a free, no-obligation quote and, when you are happy with it, we confirm the booking.
