What is a group tour in Morocco?

A group tour, also called a shared tour, is a trip where several travellers share the vehicle, the driver and the local guide. The itinerary and the dates are set, and the cost is split, which is why it is usually cheaper per person than a private tour. A small group tour keeps the number of travellers limited, so the vehicle is comfortable and the guide can give attention to everyone. A private group tour is different: only the people you travel with are in the vehicle, and you choose the pace and the stops.

Who joins a group tour?

  • Solo travellers: share the cost and meet other people on the road.
  • Couples: a lower price per person, with the same night in the Sahara.
  • Friends and family: travel together in your own private group, with a vehicle that fits your size.
  • Budget travellers: keep the cost down without giving up the guide and the camp.
  • First-time visitors: a set programme removes the work of planning.

A sample 3-day group route: Marrakech to Merzouga

Day 1: Marrakech to the Dades Valley

Leave Marrakech in the morning, cross the High Atlas by the Tizi n'Tichka pass, visit the UNESCO-listed ksar of Ait Ben Haddou and stop in Ouarzazate. Continue to the Dades Valley for the night.

Day 2: The Todra Gorges and the dunes

Visit the Todra Gorges, drive across the desert plains to Merzouga and ride a camel at sunset into the Erg Chebbi dunes. Dinner and a night in a desert camp, with a campfire and the stars.

Day 3: Back to Marrakech

Watch the sunrise over the dunes, have breakfast at camp and drive back. The days are long, so bring water and a light layer. If you prefer a slower pace, a longer tour has more time in each place. See the full 3-day tour.

Group tour or private tour? A quick checklist

  • Choose a group tour if: you travel alone or as a couple, you want to keep costs down and you are happy with a set programme.
  • Choose a private tour if: you travel as a family or with friends, you want your own pace and you value privacy.
  • Choose a private group if: you are four or more people, since the price per person falls as the group grows.
  • Not sure?: tell us your dates, your budget and who travels, and we recommend the best option.

What to check before you book a group tour

  • Group size: ask how many travellers share the vehicle.
  • The vehicle: check its type and the space for luggage.
  • What is included: meals, drinks, accommodation, activities and entrance fees.
  • The camp: ask for the type of camp and tent.
  • Driving time: ask how many hours you drive each day.
  • Guide language: confirm that the guide speaks your language.
  • How changes work: find out what happens if the group cannot form on your date.

Our guide to what to check before booking a Sahara tour and our guide on how to choose a Morocco tour company explain each point in detail.

What is included in a group tour

Each tour page lists what is included and not included. Usually the driver, the local guide, accommodation, a camel ride and the night in the camp are included, while lunches, drinks, tips and entrance fees are not. Details such as the type of camp and the meals are confirmed in your quote, so you know exactly what you pay for before you book.

What decides the price of a group tour

Prices are on request, because they depend on the tour, the dates and the size of the group. The more travellers share the vehicle and the guide, the lower the price per person. The season and the type of camp also matter. A free quote shows the price and what is included, so you can compare options.

Your own private group

If you travel with friends, family or colleagues, we organise a private tour for your group, with a vehicle that fits your size, and the price per person falls as the group grows. This gives you the cost advantage of a group and the privacy and flexibility of a private tour. Read our guide to Morocco tour cost and see our Morocco tour packages.

Tips for travelling in a group

  • Be on time: a group moves at the pace of the slowest person, so be ready at the agreed time.
  • Pack light: a small night bag for the camp keeps the vehicle organised.
  • Share your needs: tell us about food needs, health and mobility before the trip.
  • Be flexible: weather and roads can change the plan, and the guide adapts it.
  • Respect others: keep the camp quiet at night and share the best seats.
  • Bring cash: for tips, drinks and small purchases.

Best time for a group tour in Morocco

Spring and autumn are the most comfortable seasons for the Sahara. Summer is very hot in the south, and winter nights in the desert are cold. Group dates can fill up in busy periods, so ask early. See our best time to visit Morocco guide.

Small group tours Morocco: frequently asked questions

What is a small group tour in Morocco?

A small group tour is a trip where a limited number of travellers share the vehicle, the driver and the local guide, with a set programme. Because the cost is split, it usually costs less per person than a private tour. The exact group size depends on the tour and the dates, and we confirm it in your quote.

What is the difference between a shared tour and a private tour?

On a shared tour you travel with other people and follow a set programme. On a private tour only your own group travels, and you can change the pace and the stops. Shared tours usually cost less per person, and private tours give you more flexibility and privacy.

Are group tours cheaper than private tours in Morocco?

Usually yes, per person, because the cost of the vehicle and the guide is split between more travellers. The price depends on the tour, the dates and the group size, so ask for a free quote for your dates.

Is a group tour good for solo travellers?

Yes. Solo travellers often choose a group tour because they share the cost and meet other people on the road. Tell us your dates and the tour you like, and we tell you what we can arrange.

How big are the groups?

Group size depends on the tour and the dates, and we confirm it in your quote. If a small group matters to you, tell us when you ask for a quote.

Which tours are popular with groups?

The most popular are the 3-day Marrakech to Merzouga desert tour, the 2-day Marrakech to Merzouga tour, the 3-day Marrakech to Zagora tour and the 3-day Marrakech to Fes tour. All of them include a night in a Sahara camp.

Can I travel with my own group of friends or family?

Yes. If you come with friends, family or colleagues, we organise a private tour for your group, with a vehicle that fits your size. The more travellers, the lower the price per person.

What is included in a group tour?

Each tour page lists what is included, such as the driver, the guide, accommodation, the camel ride and the camp. Lunches, drinks, tips and entrance fees are usually not included. We confirm everything in your quote.

Can I book a shared tour for a specific date?

Tell us your dates and the number of travellers. We check what is possible and reply with a free quote. If we cannot form a group on your dates, we tell you honestly and suggest an alternative.

What should I check before booking a group tour?

Ask about the group size, the vehicle, what is included, the type of camp and how long you drive each day. Our guide to what to check before booking a Sahara tour explains each point.

How do I book a group tour with Highlights Morocco Tours?

Send us your dates and number of travellers with the contact form, by WhatsApp or by email. We reply with a free, no-obligation quote and, when you are happy with it, we confirm the booking.
