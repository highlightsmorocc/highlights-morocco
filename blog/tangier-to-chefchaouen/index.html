Tangier to Chefchaouen

How to get from Tangier to the blue city: distance, travel time, bus, taxi and private driver, the best stops and a day trip plan.

The short answer

Chefchaouen is about 115 km from Tangier, a drive of roughly 2 to 2.5 hours through the Rif mountains via Tetouan. There is no train, so you go by private driver, bus, grand taxi or rental car. A private driver is the most comfortable and lets you stop in Tetouan on the way, while the bus is the cheapest. You can visit the blue city as a long day trip, but one night is much better.

Chefchaouen is one of the most photographed towns in Morocco, with blue-washed lanes, mountain views and a calm pace that feels very different from Marrakech. Tangier is the closest big city and the most common arrival point for travellers coming from Spain, so Tangier to Chefchaouen is one of the most searched routes in northern Morocco. This guide explains each option, the best stops and a simple plan. When you want help, message us on WhatsApp and we organise it for you.

There is no railway line to Chefchaouen, so even if you arrive in Tangier by the Al Boraq high-speed train, you continue by road. Read about the city in our guide to things to do in Tangier.

Which option is best?

  • If you have luggage or a family: take a private driver; you load the car and go straight to the blue city.
  • If you want to see more than one place: choose a private driver or a tour, so you can stop in Tetouan and at viewpoints.
  • If you travel alone on a small budget: take the bus and arrive with a light bag.
  • If you like adventure: a shared grand taxi is the local way to travel, but agree the price and the route before you go.
  • If you are arriving by ferry: ask for a transfer from the port, so you do not need to go into the city first.

From Tangier to Chefchaouen by private driver

A private driver is the easiest option. We pick you up at your hotel, the airport, the station or the port, and drive through the Rif mountains with stops where you like. The road passes Tetouan, climbs through green hills and descends to the blue town. You travel at your own pace, with air conditioning, space for your luggage and a driver who knows the road. It is also the best way to continue from Chefchaouen to Fes or Rabat without going back. See our private tours from Tangier.

From Tangier to Chefchaouen by bus

Buses are the budget option. Direct services connect Tangier and Chefchaouen on most days, and other journeys change at Tetouan. The trip is slower than a private car and timetables change with the season, so check the departure times at the bus station or with the operator before you travel. Arrive early, keep your valuables with you and put your large bag in the hold. The bus station in Chefchaouen is a short taxi ride from the medina.

From Tangier to Chefchaouen by grand taxi

Grand taxis are shared old Mercedes cars that run on fixed routes. There is often no direct taxi from Tangier to Chefchaouen, so you may take one to Tetouan and change for another. Taxis leave when they are full, which can mean waiting. You can pay for all the seats and leave sooner, and you should always agree the price before you start.

Best stops on the way

  • Tetouan: a white city with a UNESCO-listed medina, Andalusian architecture and a good place for lunch.
  • Martil and Cabo Negro: beaches on the Mediterranean coast, useful in summer for a swim.
  • Rif viewpoints: roadside stops with views of green mountains, olive trees and small villages.
  • Talassemtane National Park: forest and mountain paths near Chefchaouen for walkers.
  • Akchour: waterfalls and the God Bridge, a popular hike from the Chefchaouen area.

Our drivers stop wherever you like, and we can shape the route to your time and interests. If you want help choosing, read our Chefchaouen guide.

A day trip plan from Tangier

  • Early morning: leave Tangier by 8:00 so you arrive in Chefchaouen at about 10:30.
  • Late morning: walk through the medina, the Plaza Uta el-Hammam and the old kasbah.
  • Lunch: try a tagine or a goat cheese salad in a terrace cafe.
  • Afternoon: climb to a viewpoint, shop for crafts and take photos in the blue lanes.
  • Late afternoon: start the drive back so you reach Tangier in the evening.

This works, but it is a long day. If you can, stay a night, and plan to be in the medina early in the morning when the lanes are quiet and the light is soft.

Should you stay the night in Chefchaouen?

Yes, if your schedule allows. The blue city is at its best early in the morning and at sunset, when most day visitors have left. A small guesthouse or riad in or near the medina gives you time to relax, eat well and explore at your own pace. We include Chefchaouen on our multi-day private tours, with hotels or riads booked for you.

What to see in Chefchaouen

The heart of the town is the medina, a small area of steep lanes, blue steps, doors and pots of flowers that you can explore on foot in a few hours. The main square, Plaza Uta el-Hammam, has cafes, a large kasbah with a small museum and a garden, and the Great Mosque with its octagonal tower. The best views are from the hill with the Spanish Mosque, a short walk above the town, which is popular at sunset. Near the medina, the Ras el-Maa spring is where locals wash clothes and families walk in the evening.

  • The medina lanes: wander without a map and look for the best doors, stairs and corners.
  • Plaza Uta el-Hammam: the main square, good for a coffee and for people watching.
  • The kasbah and its garden: a quiet courtyard and a small museum inside the old fortress.
  • Spanish Mosque viewpoint: a short climb for a view over the whole blue town.
  • Local crafts: woven blankets, wool goods, leather and natural soaps are good buys.

Going to Chefchaouen from the port, airport or station

Most travellers start at one of four places. If you arrive at Tangier Ibn Battouta Airport, a private driver can wait for you and drive straight to Chefchaouen, which is useful if your flight lands in the morning. If you arrive at the port in Tangier or at Tanger Med by ferry from Spain, your driver can meet you at the terminal and avoid a taxi into town. If you come by the Al Boraq train, the station in Tangier is a short ride from the old town and from the bus station. Whatever your arrival point, tell us your flight, ferry or train time and we adjust the pick-up if it is late.

Practical tips for the trip

  • Start early: the road is quiet in the morning and Chefchaouen is calmer before the day-trip buses arrive.
  • Bring a layer: the mountains are cooler than the coast, even in summer evenings.
  • Wear comfortable shoes: the medina is full of stairs and uneven stones.
  • Ask before taking photos of people: many locals prefer not to be photographed, so be polite.
  • Carry some cash: small cafes and shops may not take cards.
  • Check road and weather news in winter: rain or fog can slow the mountain road.

As you can see, Tangier is the closest big city, which is why many travellers combine Tangier and Chefchaouen at the start or the end of their trip. If you are travelling the whole country, our private tours link the north with Fes, the Sahara and Marrakech, so you do not repeat the road.

Is the trip worth it?

Yes. Chefchaouen is small, so you can see the main sights quickly, but its colours, mountain setting and relaxed atmosphere make it one of the most memorable stops in northern Morocco. If you only have one day in the north, spend it here, and if you have two or three days, add Tetouan, Asilah or the Akchour waterfalls. Many travellers say the extra night was the best decision of their trip, because the town feels completely different in the early morning and in the evening.

Best time to go

Spring and autumn are the best seasons, with mild days for walking. In summer it is warm and busy, and in winter it can be cold, wet and foggy in the mountains. For the whole country, read our best time to visit Morocco guide. Before you travel, check the visa and entry requirements.

Tangier to Chefchaouen: frequently asked questions

How far is Chefchaouen from Tangier?

Chefchaouen is about 115 km from Tangier. By road it takes roughly 2 to 2.5 hours without long stops, going through the Rif mountains by way of Tetouan.

How do I get from Tangier to Chefchaouen?

You can go by private driver, by bus, by grand taxi or by rental car. There is no train to Chefchaouen. A private driver is the most comfortable and flexible, and the bus is the cheapest option.

Is there a direct bus from Tangier to Chefchaouen?

Yes, direct buses run between Tangier and Chefchaouen on most days, and some journeys change in Tetouan. Journey times and timetables vary by season, so check the departure times at the bus station or with the operator before you travel.

Is there a train from Tangier to Chefchaouen?

No. Chefchaouen has no railway station. You can take the high-speed train between Tangier and other cities, but for Chefchaouen you continue by bus, taxi or private car.

Can I visit Chefchaouen as a day trip from Tangier?

Yes, it is possible, with an early start. You spend around 2 to 2.5 hours each way, which leaves 4 to 5 hours in the blue city. A night in Chefchaouen is more relaxed and lets you see the medina at sunset and early morning.

How long should I spend in Chefchaouen?

One full day covers the medina, the main viewpoints and a relaxed lunch. If you like walking, add a second day for the Akchour waterfalls and the mountains of the Talassemtane National Park.

What is the best time to visit Chefchaouen?

Spring and autumn are the most comfortable, with mild weather for walking. Summer is warm and busy, and winter can be cold and rainy in the mountains, so bring a warm layer.

Why is Chefchaouen blue?

Several stories explain it, including the tradition of Jewish refugees who settled in the town and painted the walls blue, and the belief that the colour keeps insects away and keeps houses cool. Today it is a big part of the charm of the medina.

Can I go from the Spain ferry to Chefchaouen the same day?

Yes. Many travellers arrive by ferry from Tarifa or Algeciras and continue the same day. We can meet you at the port in Tangier or at Tanger Med and take you straight to Chefchaouen with a private driver.

What can I see on the way from Tangier to Chefchaouen?

Good stops are Tetouan with its UNESCO-listed medina, the beaches near Martil and Cabo Negro, viewpoints in the Rif mountains and small roadside cafes. Our private drivers can stop wherever you like.
