The short answer

The best way to cross Morocco from Tangier to Marrakech is a private one-way tour. Our 5-day route goes from Tangier to Chefchaouen, Fes, a night in the Sahara at Merzouga and the Dades Valley, and ends in Marrakech over the High Atlas. The 8-day route adds a day in Tangier, a full day in Fes and Ait Ben Haddou, for a more relaxed pace. Both include a private driver and a local guide who speaks English or Spanish, and the price is on request, with a free quote and no obligation.

If you arrive in Tangier by plane or by ferry from Spain, and you finish in Marrakech, a one-way tour saves you from returning along the same road. In a few days you see the coast, the Rif mountains, the blue town of Chefchaouen, the imperial cities, the dunes of the Sahara and the kasbahs of the south. This guide shows the 5-day and 8-day routes day by day, compares them, explains how to start after a ferry or a flight, what is included and how to book. The 5-day Tangier to Marrakech route

Day 1: Tangier to Chefchaouen via Tetouan (about 120 km)

Meet your driver and local guide in Tangier and leave in the morning. Stop in Tetouan, a UNESCO-listed medina with Andalusian roots, and continue into the Rif mountains to Chefchaouen. Walk the blue lanes in the evening and spend the night there.

Day 2: Chefchaouen to Fes via Volubilis and Meknes (about 300 km with stops)

Enjoy the blue lanes in the morning, then drive south to Volubilis, the Roman site listed by UNESCO in 1997, and stop in Meknes, an imperial city with the Bab Mansour gate. Arrive in Fes in the evening.

Day 3: Fes to Merzouga (about 460 km)

Cross the Middle Atlas with its cedar forests, stop for lunch near Midelt and follow the Ziz Valley to the south-east. Reach the Erg Chebbi dunes in the late afternoon, ride a camel at sunset and spend the night in a desert camp.

Day 4: Merzouga to the Dades Valley (about 300 km)

Watch the sunrise over the dunes, have breakfast and drive west to Tinghir to see the Todra Gorges. Continue along the Dades Valley, with its rock formations and kasbahs, for dinner and the night.

Day 5: Dades Valley to Marrakech (about 330 km)

Drive west, stop in Ouarzazate and, if you like, visit Ait Ben Haddou, the UNESCO-listed ksar. Cross the High Atlas by the Tizi n'Tichka pass and arrive in Marrakech in the evening. See the 5-day Tangier to Marrakech tour.

The 8-day Tangier to Marrakech route

The 8-day tour follows the same line with more time. It starts with a full day in Tangier, with the medina, the kasbah, Cap Spartel and the Caves of Hercules, then continues through Tetouan to Chefchaouen. After Volubilis and Meknes, you spend a full day in Fes with a local guide, visiting the Chouara tanneries, the old madrasas and the souks. Then comes the long drive through the Middle Atlas to Merzouga and a night in the Sahara, the Dades Valley, Ait Ben Haddou and Marrakech. Read our things to do in Tangier and things to do in Fes to see what is on the route. See the 8-day tour from Tangier.

Arriving in Tangier: ferry, plane or train

  • By ferry from Spain: ferries cross from Tarifa and Algeciras to Tangier or to Tanger Med; tell us which port and the time, and we meet you.
  • By plane: Tangier Ibn Battouta Airport is about 15 km from the city, and a driver can wait for you.
  • By train: the Al Boraq high-speed train arrives from Rabat and Casablanca.

Before you travel, read our guides to the visa and entry requirements and hotels in Tangier if you want to arrive a day early.

Ending in Marrakech

The tour finishes in Marrakech, so you can fly home from there or stay a few days. It is the perfect place for a rest: a hammam, a cooking class or a balloon at sunrise. See our activities in Marrakech. If you need to return to Tangier, we can arrange a transfer or help with a train or a flight.

Highlights by stop

  • Tangier: the medina, the kasbah, Cap Spartel and the Caves of Hercules; see our Tangier guide.
  • Tetouan: a UNESCO-listed medina with Andalusian roots, on the way to the mountains.
  • Chefchaouen: the blue town in the Rif; see our Chefchaouen guide.
  • Volubilis and Meknes: Roman ruins and an imperial city with a grand gate.
  • Fes: the medina of Fes el-Bali, the tanneries and the madrasas.
  • The Sahara at Merzouga: the Erg Chebbi dunes, a camel ride and a night in camp; see things to do in Merzouga.
  • Todra Gorges and Dades Valley: tall canyon walls and rock formations.
  • Ait Ben Haddou: a UNESCO-listed ksar of red-earth towers; see Ait Ben Haddou.
  • Marrakech: the medina, the squares and the souks at the end of the trip.

Why choose a one-way tour

A one-way tour saves time and money in distance. Instead of returning to the starting point, you finish in a different city, which is ideal if you arrive in Tangier and fly home from Marrakech. It also lets you see more new places, because you do not repeat the same road. The drivers and guides know the route, the stops and the best places to eat, and your luggage stays in the same vehicle for the whole trip. If you need to go back to Tangier, we can arrange a transfer or help you with a train or a flight.

Best time for the Tangier to Marrakech tour

Spring and autumn are the best seasons, with warm days and cool nights. Summer is very hot in the south and the Sahara, and winter nights are cold, with snow in the Atlas on some days. See our best time to visit Morocco guide.

What to pack

  • Layers: the north is cool, the south is hot, and the desert is cold at night.
  • Comfortable shoes: for the medinas and the dunes.
  • Modest clothes: for cities and mosques; see our Moroccan clothing guide.
  • A small night bag: for the camp.
  • Sun protection: a hat, sunglasses and sun cream.

For more tips, read our Morocco travel tips.

Tangier to Marrakech tour: frequently asked questions

Can I do a tour from Tangier to Marrakech?

Yes. We offer private one-way tours from Tangier to Marrakech through Tetouan, Chefchaouen, Fes, a night in the Sahara and the Dades Valley, with a private driver and a local guide who speaks English or Spanish.

How many days do I need from Tangier to Marrakech?

Five days is the shortest route that includes Chefchaouen, Fes, the Sahara and Marrakech. Eight days adds a day in Tangier and a full day in Fes, and gives a more relaxed pace. Longer 10 and 14-day tours add more places.

Which cities does the tour visit?

The route visits Tangier or Tetouan, Chefchaouen, Volubilis and Meknes, Fes, the Erg Chebbi dunes at Merzouga, the Todra Gorges and Dades Valley, Ait Ben Haddou and Marrakech.

Is the tour private?

Yes. It is a private tour with your own vehicle, driver and guide. You can adapt the pace and the stops.

Can I start the tour after arriving by ferry from Spain?

Yes. Many travellers arrive in Tangier by ferry from Tarifa or Algeciras, or by plane, and start the tour the same day or the next morning. Tell us your arrival time and we meet you at the port or airport.

Does the tour include a night in the Sahara?

Yes. Both the 5-day and the 8-day tours include a camel ride at sunset in the Erg Chebbi dunes and a night in a desert camp at Merzouga.

Where does the tour end?

The tour ends in Marrakech. If you need to return to Tangier, we can arrange a transfer or help you with a train or a flight.

How much does the tour from Tangier to Marrakech cost?

The price is on request, because it depends on your dates, the length and the number of travellers. We send a free quote with no obligation.

What is the best time of year?

Spring and autumn are the most comfortable. Summer is very hot in the south, and winter nights in the desert are cold, with snow in the Atlas on some days.

Can I do the tour in the other direction?

Yes. We also offer a 5-day tour from Marrakech to Tangier via Merzouga. Tell us which direction suits your flights or ferry.

How do I book the Tangier to Marrakech tour?

Send us your dates and number of travellers with the contact form, by WhatsApp or by email. We reply with a free, no-obligation quote and, when you are happy with it, we confirm the booking.
