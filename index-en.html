Before you travel

Practical information for your Morocco trip

Check these general tips to prepare your trip through Marrakech, the Sahara and other destinations in Morocco.

Visa

Entry requirements depend on your nationality and trip length. Confirm current conditions with the embassy or consulate.

Currency

The local currency is the Moroccan dirham (MAD). Card acceptance can vary, so it is useful to have some cash available.

Internet & SIM

You can check local SIM or eSIM options. Before you travel, confirm your phone is compatible and review your operator rates.

What to bring

Include comfortable shoes, sunscreen and a light layer for cooler nights. Adapt clothing to the season and locations.

Languages

Arabic and Amazigh are official languages. French is also widely spoken, and in tourist areas Spanish and English are common.
