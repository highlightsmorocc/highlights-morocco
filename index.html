About Us

Why travel with Highlights Morocco Tours?

Meet Moha, from the Moroccan desert. Highlights Morocco Tours is based in Marrakech, and every trip we organise is built around the people travelling: your dates, your pace and the places you really want to see.

You travel with a private driver and a local guide who speaks English or Spanish. We arrange riads in Marrakech and camps in Merzouga, and if your plans change along the way, you tell us and we adjust. We are available 24/7 while you are in Morocco.

Ask on WhatsApp

Are your private Morocco tours truly private?

Yes. Our private Morocco tours are planned around your dates, interests and pace, with a private driver and a local guide.

Which cities do your Morocco tours and day trips start from?

Our multi-day Morocco tours start from Marrakech, Casablanca, Fes, Rabat, Tangier and Errachidia. Day trips start from Marrakech, Fes, Ouarzazate and Merzouga.

Do you offer Merzouga desert tours from Marrakech?

Yes. We offer 2, 3 and 4-day Merzouga desert tours from Marrakech, with a night in a Sahara desert camp. The 3-day tour includes a stop at Ait Ben Haddou and the Todra Gorges.

Can I go camel trekking in the Sahara desert?

Yes. A camel ride at sunset is part of our Sahara desert experiences, with nights under the stars in a desert camp. Ask us for the details of the tour you prefer.

What day trips do you offer from Marrakech?

Popular day trips from Marrakech include the Ourika Valley, the Ouzoud Waterfalls and Ait Ben Haddou. Each one returns to Marrakech the same day.

How do I get a free quote for a Morocco tour?

Use the search bar or the contact form on this page, or write to us by email or WhatsApp. Quotes are free and come with no obligation.

Can you tailor a private Morocco itinerary to my plans?

Yes. Tell us your dates, group size and what you would like to see, and we will build a tailor-made itinerary for couples, families or groups.

Do your guides speak English and Spanish?

Yes. Our local guides speak English and Spanish, and we offer support before and during your trip.

Where do we stay on a Sahara desert tour?

You can stay in a desert camp in Merzouga, and we can arrange riads in Marrakech. Tell us your preferences and we will suggest options.

What should I pack for a Sahara desert tour?

Comfortable shoes, sun protection and light clothes for the day, plus a warm layer for the evening, as desert nights can be cool.

Do I need a visa to travel to Morocco?

Entry requirements depend on your nationality and the length of your stay. Please check the current rules with the official Moroccan authorities before you travel.
