About Us

Why travel with Highlights Morocco Tours?

Meet Moha, from the Moroccan desert. Highlights Morocco Tours is based in Marrakech, and every trip we organise is built around the people travelling: your dates, your pace and the places you really want to see.

You travel with a private driver and a local guide who speaks English or Spanish. We arrange riads in Marrakech and camps in Merzouga, and if your plans change along the way, you tell us and we adjust. We are available 24/7 while you are in Morocco.

Not a fixed script. Your Morocco, at your own pace.
