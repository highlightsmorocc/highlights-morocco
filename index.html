Practical info

Before you travel

Visa

Confirm your entry requirements based on nationality and duration of stay.

Currency

The local currency is the Moroccan dirham (MAD), so keep cash available.

Internet

SIMs and eSIMs are easy to find, and mobile data is widely available.

What to bring

Comfortable shoes, sunscreen, layers and light clothes for warm nights.

Languages

Arabic and Amazigh are official, while French and English are common in travel areas.
