Merzouga or Zagora?

Compare the two most popular Sahara desert routes and choose the best experience for your Morocco trip.

Merzouga

Distance from Marrakech: Approximately 560 km (2-3 days)

Duration: Typically 3-4 days

Desert: Erg Chebbi - the most iconic Sahara dunes

Experience: Larger dunes (up to 160m), camel trekking, Berber camps, sunrise and sunset in the desert

Best for: First-time desert visitors, families, those wanting the classic Sahara experience

Iconic Popular Photos

Zagora

Distance from Marrakech: Approximately 470 km (2 days)

Duration: Typically 2-3 days

Desert: Tinfou and Erg Murzuq - smaller, more accessible dunes

Experience: Oasis villages, Draa Valley, camel walks, quieter atmosphere, closer to civilization

Best for: Short trips, budget travelers, those seeking a quieter desert experience
