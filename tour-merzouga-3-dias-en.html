3-Day Merzouga Tour

Cross the High Atlas, visit the kasbah of Ait Ben Haddou, explore Todra Gorges and spend an unforgettable night in the Sahara desert.

Duration

3 days / 2 nights

Group Size

Private tour (1-8 people)

Price

€290 per person

Itinerary

Day 1: Marrakech → Ait Ben Haddou → Ouarzazate

  • ✓ Morning departure from your hotel in Marrakech
  • ✓ Cross the High Atlas mountains (pass 2260m)
  • ✓ Visit the UNESCO kasbah of Ait Ben Haddou
  • ✓ Lunch at a traditional Moroccan restaurant
  • ✓ Arrive in Ouarzazate and check into your hotel
  • ✓ Dinner and overnight in Ouarzazate

Day 2: Ouarzazate → Todra Gorges → Merzouga

  • ✓ Breakfast and departure towards Merzouga
  • ✓ Stop at Todra Gorges for trekking and photos
  • ✓ Lunch in a local village
  • ✓ Continue to Merzouga
  • ✓ Camel ride at sunset on Erg Chebbi dunes
  • ✓ Dinner and overnight in a Berber camp

Day 3: Merzouga → Marrakech

  • ✓ Early morning camel ride for the desert sunrise
  • ✓ Breakfast at the camp
  • ✓ Return to Marrakech (direct route or scenic stops)
  • ✓ Arrive in Marrakech in the afternoon
  • ✓ Drop-off at your hotel

What's Included

  • Private transportation in a comfortable 4x4
  • Expert local guide (Spanish/English)
  • 2 nights accommodation (hotel + desert camp)
  • All meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner)
  • Camel trekking in the desert
  • Activities and entrance fees

What's Not Included

  • Travel from your home to Marrakech
  • Travel insurance
  • Personal expenses and tips
  • Optional activities
