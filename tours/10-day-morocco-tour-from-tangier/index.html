10-Day Morocco Tour from Tangier to Marrakech

A private one-way tour of 10 days from Tangier to Marrakech: Chefchaouen, Fes, a night in the Sahara at Merzouga, the Dades Valley and Essaouira, with a driver and a local guide.

Tour overview

Cross Morocco from Tangier in 10 days

The 10-day Morocco tour from Tangier is a private one-way tour by Highlights Morocco Tours. It starts in Tangier, continues through Tetouan to Chefchaouen with a free day, visits Volubilis, Meknes and Fes, spends a night in a Sahara desert camp at Merzouga, follows the Dades Valley and Ait Ben Haddou to Marrakech and adds Essaouira before the end of the tour in Marrakech. It includes a private driver and a local guide who speaks English or Spanish. The price is on request, with a free quote and no obligation.

Ten days is a comfortable length to see the best of Morocco without rushing. If you arrive in Tangier by plane or by ferry from Spain, this route crosses the country in one line, from the north to Marrakech, without going back over the same road. You see the coast, the Rif, the imperial cities, the desert and the Atlas.

It is a typical route, not a fixed script. We adapt the stops and nights to your dates and pace, and we can add days in the desert, in Fes, in Marrakech or on the coast. For a shorter trip see our 8-day Morocco tours, and for a longer one our 14-day Morocco tours.

Itinerary

Day by day itinerary

A typical 10-day one-way trip from Tangier. Stops and timings are adapted to you.

Day1

Tangier

Tangier

  • Arrive in Tangier and meet your driver and local guide.
  • Visit the medina and the kasbah, with views over the Strait of Gibraltar.
  • Drive to Cap Spartel, where the Atlantic meets the Mediterranean, and visit the Caves of Hercules.
  • Overnight in Tangier.

Entrance fees and the exact stops are confirmed in your quote.

Day2

Tangier to Chefchaouen via Tetouan

About 120 km

  • Leave Tangier and drive to Tetouan, a UNESCO-listed medina with Andalusian roots.
  • Continue into the Rif mountains to Chefchaouen.
  • Walk the blue lanes of the old town in the evening.

The drive is short, so there is time to explore.

Day3

Chefchaouen: free day

Chefchaouen

  • Enjoy a free day in the blue town of the Rif mountains.
  • Walk the old town and the kasbah at your own pace, or take the path up to the viewpoint over the town.
  • If you like walking, ask us about an excursion to the Akchour waterfalls.

Optional excursions are confirmed in your quote.

Day4

Chefchaouen to Fes via Volubilis and Meknes

About 300 km with stops

  • Enjoy the blue lanes of Chefchaouen in the morning.
  • Drive south and visit Volubilis, the Roman site listed by UNESCO in 1997.
  • Stop in Meknes, an imperial city with the Bab Mansour gate.
  • Arrive in Fes in the evening.

We can shorten the day by skipping Meknes or Volubilis.

Day5

A day in Fes

Fes el-Bali

  • Spend the day with a local guide in the medina of Fes el-Bali, a UNESCO World Heritage site (listed in 1981).
  • Visit the Chouara tanneries, the old madrasas and the souks.
  • Enjoy a traditional lunch and free time in the evening.

Entrance fees and the lunch are confirmed in your quote.

Day6

Fes to Merzouga

About 460 km

  • Leave Fes in the morning and cross the Middle Atlas, with its cedar forests.
  • Stop for lunch near Midelt and follow the Ziz Valley to the south-east.
  • Reach the Erg Chebbi dunes in the late afternoon and ride a camel at sunset.
  • Dinner, music and a night in a desert camp beside the dunes.

This is a long driving day. The camel ride depends on the season and your arrival time.

Day7

Merzouga to the Dades Valley

About 300 km

  • Watch the sunrise over the dunes and have breakfast at camp.
  • Drive west towards Tinghir and visit the Todra Gorges.
  • Continue along the Dades Valley, with its rock formations and kasbahs.
  • Dinner and night in the Dades Valley.

Stops in the Merzouga area, such as Rissani, can be added. Tell us what you prefer.

Day8

Dades Valley to Marrakech

About 330 km

  • Leave the Dades Valley after breakfast and drive west.
  • Stop in Ouarzazate and visit Ait Ben Haddou.
  • Cross the High Atlas by the Tizi n'Tichka pass.
  • Arrive in Marrakech in the evening.

The pass can be slow in winter weather. We adapt the stops on the day.

Day9

Marrakech to Essaouira

About 175 km

  • Leave Marrakech in the morning and drive west to the Atlantic coast.
  • Walk the ramparts, the medina and the fishing port of Essaouira.
  • Enjoy fresh seafood in the evening.

Essaouira can be windy. Bring a light jacket.

Day10

Essaouira to Marrakech

About 175 km

  • Stroll the medina and the fishing port in the morning.
  • Drive back to Marrakech in the afternoon.
  • Free evening in Marrakech.

Arrival time depends on the stops you choose.

Good to know

Tips for your trip

  • The route has several long driving days, mainly between Merzouga and the cities. We plan stops for lunch and rest, and we can add a night to slow down.
  • Spring and autumn are the most comfortable seasons. Summer is very hot inland and winter desert nights are cold. Check the weather forecast a few days before you travel.
  • Bring light layers, a warm jacket for the desert night, a scarf, sunglasses, sun cream and closed shoes.
  • Want to slow down or add a day? Ask us to add a night in Fes, Marrakech, Essaouira or the desert.

FAQ

Questions about the 10-day tour

About the trip, how to book it and who we are.

The tour

Is 10 days in Morocco enough?

Yes. Ten days is a good length to see the highlights without rushing: the imperial cities, the blue town of Chefchaouen, the Atlas and a night in the Sahara, with a private driver and a local guide.

What is the best 10-day Morocco itinerary from Tangier?

A popular 10-day itinerary from Tangier goes Tetouan, Chefchaouen, Fes, Merzouga, the Dades Valley, Ait Ben Haddou, Marrakech and Essaouira. It never repeats a road and includes a night in the Sahara.

How much driving is there on a 10-day Morocco tour?

Morocco is big, so there are some long driving days, mostly between Merzouga and Fes. We plan stops for lunch and rest, and we can add a night to slow down.

Does the 10-day tour include the Sahara?

Yes. It includes a camel ride at sunset in the Erg Chebbi dunes and a night in a desert camp at Merzouga. The camel ride depends on the season and your arrival time.

Can I change the route or add days?

Yes. We adapt the stops and the number of nights to your dates and interests, and we can add a night in the desert, in Fes, in Marrakech or on the coast.

Is this tour one way?

Yes. It starts in Tangier and ends in Marrakech. If you need to return to Tangier, we can arrange a transfer or help you with a train or a flight.

Can you pick me up at the Tangier port or airport?

Yes. We can arrange a private pick-up at the port, the airport or your hotel in Tangier. Tell us your arrival time and we confirm it in your quote.

Booking

How do I book the 10-day Morocco tour from Tangier?

Send us your dates and number of travellers with the contact form, by WhatsApp or by email. We reply with a free, no-obligation quote and, once you are happy with it, we confirm the booking and the details of your trip.

How much does the 10-day tour cost?

The price depends on your dates, the number of travellers and the type of camp, so we quote on request. Quotes are free and come with no obligation.

Where does the tour start and end?

The tour starts in Tangier and ends in Marrakech. We can arrange the pick-up at the Tangier port, the airport or your hotel, and we agree the time with you when we confirm your booking.
