Tour overview

A short escape to the Sahara

The 2-day Marrakech to Merzouga desert tour is a private Morocco tour package by Highlights Morocco Tours. You travel from Marrakech over the High Atlas, past Ait Ben Haddou, to the Erg Chebbi dunes at Merzouga, spend one night in a desert camp and return to Marrakech on day 2. It includes a private driver and a local guide who speaks English or Spanish. The price is on request, with a free quote and no obligation.

This tour is for travellers who have little time but do not want to miss the Sahara. You leave Marrakech in the morning, cross the Atlas Mountains, see Ait Ben Haddou and the valleys of the south-east, and arrive at the dunes of Merzouga in time for the sunset.

The distance is long, about 560 km each way, so this is a trip for travellers who enjoy the road. If you prefer a slower pace, look at our 3-day and 4-day Merzouga tours from Marrakech.

The main stops of the 2-day Merzouga tour from Marrakech. We adapt the route to your plans.

Itinerary

Day by day itinerary

A typical 2-day itinerary from Marrakech to Merzouga. Stops and timings are adapted to you.

Day1

Marrakech to Merzouga

About 560 km

  • Early start from Marrakech with your private driver and local guide.
  • Cross the High Atlas by the Tizi n'Tichka pass, with photo stops on the way.
  • Visit Ait Ben Haddou, the UNESCO-listed ksar, and stop in Ouarzazate.
  • Continue along the Dades Valley and the Todra Gorges region, with a lunch stop.
  • Arrive in Merzouga in the evening and move into your desert camp.
  • Watch the sunset over the Erg Chebbi dunes, have dinner and spend the night in camp.

Want a camel trek into the dunes at sunset? Tell us when you ask for your quote so that we can plan the timing.

Day2

Merzouga to Marrakech

About 560 km

  • Wake up to the sunrise over the dunes and have breakfast at camp.
  • Say goodbye to the desert and leave Merzouga with your driver and guide.
  • Drive back towards Marrakech with stops for lunch and rest.
  • Arrive in Marrakech in the evening, at the point we agree with you.

Arrival times depend on traffic, the weather on the pass and the stops you choose.

Caravan routes and kasbahs

Along the old caravan routes

For centuries, caravans crossed the Atlas Mountains between the Sahara and Marrakech. The ksour and kasbahs you pass on this tour, such as Ait Ben Haddou, grew along these trade routes, and a caravanserai was the roadside inn where caravans and their camels could rest.

Today you follow the same road by private car with a local guide, and finish where the caravans began their journey across the dunes: in Merzouga, at the edge of the Erg Chebbi.

Good to know

Tips for your trip

  • Expect about 9 to 10 hours on the road each day. It is a long, intense itinerary.
  • Spring and autumn are the most comfortable seasons. Summer is very hot and winter desert nights are cold.
  • Bring light layers, a warm jacket, a scarf, sunglasses, sun cream and closed shoes.
  • Want a slower pace? Ask for the 3-day or 4-day Merzouga tour.

About the trip, how to book it and who we are.

The tour

Is the 2-day Marrakech to Merzouga desert tour worth it?

Yes, if you are short on time and want to see the Sahara dunes. It is an intense trip with long driving days, so many travellers choose the 3-day or 4-day tour for a slower pace. Tell us how much time you have and we will recommend the best option.

How long is the drive from Marrakech to Merzouga?

Merzouga is about 560 km from Marrakech. Allow roughly 9 to 10 hours of driving on each of the two days, including the Tizi n'Tichka pass and your stops on the way.

What will I see between Marrakech and Merzouga?

The usual route crosses the High Atlas by the Tizi n'Tichka pass and stops at Ait Ben Haddou, a UNESCO-listed ksar. It continues through Ouarzazate and the Dades Valley and Todra Gorges region before reaching the Erg Chebbi dunes at Merzouga. We adapt the stops to your pace and interests.

Where do we sleep on the 2-day desert tour?

You spend one night in a desert camp in Merzouga, close to the Erg Chebbi dunes. Tell us what kind of camp you prefer and we will confirm the details in your quote.

Can I ride a camel in Merzouga?

A camel trek in the dunes can be arranged. Tell us when you ask for your quote so that we can plan the timing around sunset or sunrise.

When is the best time to do the Marrakech to Merzouga tour?

Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) are usually the most comfortable seasons. Summer is very hot in the desert and winter nights can be cold, so pack accordingly.

What should I bring?

Light layers and a warm jacket for the desert night, a scarf, sunglasses, sun cream, comfortable closed shoes, a small bag for the camp, a phone charger and some cash for drinks and tips.

Can I make the tour longer?

Yes. We also organise a 3-day and a 4-day Marrakech to Merzouga tour with more time on the road and in the dunes, and we can tailor any route to your dates.
