Tour overview

The Sahara with a shorter drive, through the Draa Valley

The 2-day Marrakech to Zagora desert tour is a private Morocco tour package by Highlights Morocco Tours. You cross the High Atlas, visit Ait Ben Haddou and Ouarzazate, follow the palm groves of the Draa Valley to Zagora, spend one night in a desert camp and return to Marrakech on day 2. Zagora is closer than Merzouga, about 350 km from Marrakech, so the drive is shorter. It includes hotel pick-up and drop-off in Marrakech, an air-conditioned vehicle, an experienced driver and local guide, a camel ride, and a night in a private tent in a desert camp with dinner and breakfast. Lunches, drinks and monument entrances are not included. The price is on request, with a free quote and no obligation.

Zagora is the gateway to the desert of the Draa Valley, the longest river valley in Morocco. It is about 350 km from Marrakech, much closer than Merzouga, so a 2-day trip here is less tiring and leaves more time to enjoy the road, the palm groves and the evening in the desert.

The landscape is different from the tall dunes of Erg Chebbi: expect oases, kasbahs, rocky desert and smaller dunes. If you want the big golden dunes, look at our 3-day Merzouga tour. If you are comparing both, read our guide Merzouga or Zagora.

Itinerary

Day by day itinerary

A typical 2-day itinerary from Marrakech to Zagora. Stops and timings are adapted to you.

Day1

Marrakech to Zagora

About 350 km

  • Leave Marrakech in the morning with your private driver and local guide.
  • Cross the High Atlas by the Tizi n'Tichka pass, with photo stops on the way.
  • Visit Ait Ben Haddou, the UNESCO-listed ksar, and stop in Ouarzazate.
  • Follow the Draa Valley through Agdz and its palm groves, with a lunch stop.
  • Arrive in Zagora in the afternoon or evening and move into your desert camp.
  • Watch the sunset over the dunes, have dinner and spend the night in camp.

The camel ride, dinner and breakfast at the camp are included. Moroccan mint tea is served at the camp.

Day2

Zagora to Marrakech

About 350 km

  • Wake up to the sunrise over the desert and have breakfast at camp.
  • Stop at the famous sign to Timbuktu in Zagora before leaving.
  • Drive back towards Marrakech through the Draa Valley and over the High Atlas.
  • Arrive in Marrakech in the evening, at the point we agree with you.

Arrival times depend on traffic, the weather on the pass and the stops you choose. Extra stops can be added.

Caravan routes and kasbahs

Along the old caravan routes

For centuries, caravans crossed the Atlas Mountains between the Sahara and Marrakech. The ksour and kasbahs you pass on this tour, such as Ait Ben Haddou, grew along these trade routes, and a caravanserai was the roadside inn where caravans and their camels could rest.

Today you follow the same road by private car with a local guide, and finish at the edge of the desert, in the Draa Valley, where the caravans once set out towards Timbuktu.

The tour

Is the 2-day Marrakech to Zagora desert tour worth it?

Yes, if you have 2 days and prefer a shorter drive. Zagora is closer than Merzouga, so the trip is less tiring, with the Draa Valley palm groves and a night in the desert. If you want the tall dunes, the Merzouga tours are a better match.

How long is the drive from Marrakech to Zagora?

Zagora is about 350 km from Marrakech. Allow roughly 6 hours of driving each day, plus stops, including the Tizi n'Tichka pass.

What will I see between Marrakech and Zagora?

The usual route crosses the High Atlas by the Tizi n'Tichka pass and stops at Ait Ben Haddou, a UNESCO-listed ksar. It continues through Ouarzazate and Agdz along the palm groves of the Draa Valley to Zagora. We adapt the stops to your pace and interests.

Where do we sleep on the 2-day Zagora tour?

You spend one night in a private tent in a desert camp near Zagora, with dinner and breakfast included.

Can I ride a camel near Zagora?

Yes. A camel ride in the dunes is included in the tour, and we plan the timing around the sunset.

What is the difference between Zagora and Merzouga?

Zagora is closer to Marrakech and has oases, palm groves and smaller dunes. Merzouga is farther away and has the tall golden dunes of Erg Chebbi. Our guide Merzouga or Zagora explains the difference in more detail.

When is the best time to do the Marrakech to Zagora tour?

Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) are usually the most comfortable seasons. Summer is very hot in the desert and winter nights can be cold, so pack accordingly.

What is included in the 2-day Zagora tour?

Hotel pick-up and drop-off in Marrakech, a tour in an air-conditioned vehicle, an experienced driver and local guide, one night in a private tent in a desert camp with dinner and breakfast, a camel ride, Moroccan mint tea at the camp, fuel, and stops at interesting places and panoramic viewpoints on the way.

What is not included in the price?

Lunches, drinks and the entrance fees to monuments, which are optional. We confirm every detail in your free quote.

What should I bring?

Light layers and a warm jacket for the desert night, a scarf, sunglasses, sun cream, comfortable closed shoes, a small bag for the camp, a phone charger and some cash for drinks and tips.

Can I make the tour longer?

Yes. We also organise the 2, 3 and 4-day Marrakech to Merzouga tours with more time in the desert, and we can tailor any route to your dates.
