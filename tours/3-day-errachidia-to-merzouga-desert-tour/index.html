Tour overview

Three days in the Sahara from Errachidia

The 3-day desert tour from Errachidia to Merzouga is a private tour by Highlights Morocco Tours. It follows the Ziz Valley to Erfoud and Rissani, reaches the Erg Chebbi dunes at Merzouga for a camel ride at sunset and two nights in a Sahara desert camp, with a full day in the desert, and returns to Errachidia on day 3. It includes a private driver and a local guide who speaks English or Spanish. The price is on request, with a free quote and no obligation.

Errachidia is one of the closest cities to the Erg Chebbi dunes, so you spend your time in the desert and not on the road. The first day follows the Ziz Valley, with its palm groves, to Erfoud and Rissani, and reaches the dunes in time for a camel ride at sunset.

The second day is free for the desert. You can walk the dunes at sunrise, visit the Gnawa village of Khamlia and add a quad or buggy ride, a camel trek or a 4x4 trip with the nomads. We adapt it to your pace, and we can extend the tour to the Todra Gorges and the Dades Valley.

Tour guide

Complete guide to the 3-day Errachidia to Merzouga desert tour

The 3-day desert tour from Errachidia to Merzouga is a private round trip with your own driver and a local guide who speaks English or Spanish. The distance is short, about 130 km each way, so the days stay relaxed. You follow the Ziz Valley, see Erfoud and Rissani and spend two nights in a Sahara camp at Merzouga.

01

What is the 3-day Errachidia to Merzouga desert tour?

The 3-day Errachidia to Merzouga desert tour is a private Sahara tour by Highlights Morocco Tours that starts and ends in Errachidia. With your own driver and a local guide who speaks English or Spanish, you follow the Ziz Valley to Erfoud and Rissani and reach the Erg Chebbi dunes at Merzouga. The tour includes a camel ride at sunset and two nights in a desert camp, with a full day in the desert between the two drives. At about 130 km and about 2 hours each way, the road is short.

02

Day-by-day itinerary of the Errachidia to Merzouga tour

Day 1: Meet your driver and local guide in Errachidia and leave in the morning, following the Ziz Valley and its palm groves to Erfoud. Day 1 also includes a stop in Rissani, a market town on the old caravan routes, before dinner, music and a first night in the desert camp. Day 2: Watch the sunrise over the dunes, have breakfast at camp and visit Khamlia, a village known for its Gnawa music. Day 2 leaves room for an optional quad or buggy ride, a camel trek or a 4x4 trip with the nomads, then a rest, dinner and a second night in camp. Day 3: Enjoy a last sunrise, have breakfast and drive back along the Ziz Valley to Errachidia, where the tour ends.

03

Who should choose a desert tour from Errachidia?

The 3-day Errachidia to Merzouga tour suits travellers who are already in the Errachidia area, or who prefer a slow desert trip with little driving. With only about 2 hours on the road each way, the days leave plenty of room for rest, which helps families, older travellers and anyone tired of long transfers. Active visitors can fill the full day in Merzouga with a camel trek, a quad ride or a walk on the dunes. Those who want big mountain scenery on the route will find this tour quieter than the longer circuits.

04

Best time to visit Merzouga from Errachidia

For the Errachidia to Merzouga desert tour, spring and autumn are usually the most comfortable seasons, with pleasant days and cool evenings by the dunes. In summer, Erfoud, Rissani and Merzouga can be very hot, so activities are best kept for early morning and late afternoon. In winter, nights at the Sahara camp are cold, and days can be fresh and clear; a snowfall in the High Atlas does not usually affect this short route directly. Sandy wind can appear in spring, so a scarf is always useful.

05

What to pack for two nights in a Sahara camp

For two nights on the Errachidia to Merzouga tour, pack light layers for the day and a warm jacket or fleece for the camp, since the desert cools quickly after sunset. A scarf, sunglasses, a hat and sun cream protect you from sun and sand, and closed shoes are better than sandals for the dunes. The tour does not include lunches, drinks, entrance fees or tips, so bring cash for meals in Erfoud or Rissani, optional activities such as quad rides, and your driver and guide. Dinner and breakfast at camp are included.

The tour

Is the 3-day desert tour from Errachidia worth it?

Yes, if you want to spend your time in the desert and not on the road. Errachidia is about 130 km from Merzouga, so you reach the dunes the first day and have a full day in the desert.

How far is Errachidia from Merzouga?

Errachidia is about 130 km from Merzouga, which takes around 2 hours by car with stops in the Ziz Valley, Erfoud and Rissani.

Does the tour include two nights in a desert camp?

Yes. It includes two nights in a desert camp beside the Erg Chebbi dunes, with dinner and music, and a camel ride at sunset. The camel ride depends on the season and your arrival time.

What can I do on the second day?

You can watch the sunrise over the dunes, visit the Gnawa village of Khamlia and add a quad or buggy ride, a camel trek or a 4x4 trip with the nomads. Prices for activities are confirmed on request.

Can I extend the tour to the Todra Gorges and the Dades Valley?

Yes. We can add days to visit the Todra Gorges and the Dades Valley, or end the trip in another city. Tell us your plans and we send a free quote.

Can you pick me up at the Errachidia airport or station?

We can arrange the pick-up at your hotel or another agreed point in Errachidia. Tell us your arrival details and we confirm what can be arranged in your quote.
