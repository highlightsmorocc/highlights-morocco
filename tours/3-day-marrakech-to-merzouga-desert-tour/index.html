Tour overview

The classic Sahara trip at a balanced pace

The 3-day Marrakech to Merzouga desert tour is a private Morocco tour package by Highlights Morocco Tours. You cross the High Atlas, visit Ait Ben Haddou and the Todra Gorges, ride a camel at sunset in the Erg Chebbi dunes and spend a night in a Merzouga desert camp, with 2 nights in total, before returning to Marrakech. It includes a private driver and a local guide who speaks English or Spanish. The price is on request, with a free quote and no obligation.

The 3-day tour is the classic way to see the Moroccan Sahara. With one more day than the 2-day tour, the long drive is split in two, so you have time to stop at Ait Ben Haddou, walk in the Todra Gorges and reach the dunes of Merzouga in time for a camel ride at sunset.

The route follows the old caravan routes between the Sahara and Marrakech, past ksour and kasbahs that once served the caravans. If you want more time in the desert, look at our 4-day Merzouga tour, or at the shorter 2-day.

Itinerary

Day by day itinerary

A typical 3-day itinerary from Marrakech to Merzouga. Stops and timings are adapted to you.

Day1

Marrakech to the Dades Valley

Marrakech - Dades

  • Leave Marrakech in the morning with your private driver and local guide.
  • Cross the High Atlas by the Tizi n'Tichka pass.
  • Visit Ait Ben Haddou, the UNESCO-listed ksar, and stop in Ouarzazate.
  • Continue towards the Dades Valley, with a lunch stop on the way.
  • Spend the night in the Dades or Todra area.

The place for the first night is confirmed in your free quote.

Day2

Todra Gorges to Merzouga

Dades - Merzouga

  • Visit the Todra Gorges, with their tall canyon walls.
  • Drive on towards Merzouga across the desert plains of the south-east.
  • Ride a camel at sunset in the Erg Chebbi dunes.
  • Have dinner and spend the night in a Merzouga desert camp.

Camel ride timing depends on the season and your arrival time.

Day3

Merzouga to Marrakech

About 560 km

  • Watch the sunrise over the dunes and have breakfast at camp.
  • Leave Merzouga with your driver and guide.
  • Drive back to Marrakech with stops for lunch and rest.
  • Arrive in Marrakech in the evening, at the point we agree with you.

Arrival times depend on traffic, the weather on the pass and the stops you choose.

Caravan routes and kasbahs

Along the old caravan routes

For centuries, caravans crossed the Atlas Mountains between the Sahara and Marrakech. The ksour and kasbahs you pass on this tour, such as Ait Ben Haddou, grew along these trade routes, and a caravanserai was the roadside inn where caravans and their camels could rest.

Today you follow the same road by private car with a local guide, and finish where the caravans began their journey across the dunes: in Merzouga, at the edge of the Erg Chebbi.

Good to know

Tips for your trip

  • Expect long driving days, with the longest on the way back to Marrakech.
  • Spring and autumn are the most comfortable seasons. Summer is very hot and winter desert nights are cold.
  • Bring light layers, a warm jacket, a scarf, sunglasses, sun cream and closed shoes.
  • Short on time? Ask for the 2-day tour. Want more desert? Ask for the 4-day tour.

Questions about the 3-day tour

About the trip, how to book it and who we are.

The tour

Is the 3-day Marrakech to Merzouga tour the best option?

For most travellers it is a good balance. The long drive is split over three days, so you have time for Ait Ben Haddou, the Todra Gorges and a camel ride at sunset. If you have more time, the 4-day tour is more relaxed.

What is the itinerary of the 3-day tour from Marrakech to Merzouga?

Day 1: Marrakech, the Tizi n'Tichka pass, Ait Ben Haddou, Ouarzazate and the Dades Valley. Day 2: the Todra Gorges and Merzouga, with a camel ride at sunset and a night in a desert camp. Day 3: sunrise in the dunes and the drive back to Marrakech.

Where do we sleep on the 3-day desert tour?

You spend the first night on the road, in the Dades or Todra area, and the second night in a desert camp in Merzouga. We confirm the places in your quote, so tell us what you prefer.

Is a camel ride included in the 3-day tour?

The tour includes a camel ride at sunset in the Erg Chebbi dunes, which depends on the season and your arrival time. We confirm the details in your quote.

How many hours do we drive each day?

Marrakech is about 560 km from Merzouga. The drive is split over the three days, with the longest day on the way back. We plan stops for photos, lunch and rest.

When is the best time to do the 3-day Merzouga tour?

Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) are usually the most comfortable seasons. Summer is very hot in the desert and winter nights can be cold.

What should I bring?

Light layers and a warm jacket for the desert night, a scarf, sunglasses, sun cream, comfortable closed shoes, a small bag for the camp, a phone charger and some cash for drinks and tips.

Can I change the route or add days?

Yes. We can add a day or change the stops, and we also organise the 2-day and 4-day Marrakech to Merzouga tours.
