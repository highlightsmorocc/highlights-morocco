Tour overview

Time to really enjoy the desert

The 6-day Marrakech to Merzouga desert tour is a private Morocco tour package by Highlights Morocco Tours. It crosses the High Atlas through Ouarzazate and Ait Ben Haddou to the Dades Valley, spends three nights in a Sahara desert camp at Merzouga with a camel ride at sunset and two full days in the dunes, then crosses the Middle Atlas to Fes and returns to Marrakech. It includes a private driver and a local guide who speaks English or Spanish. The price is on request, with a free quote and no obligation.

The 6-day tour is for travellers who want the desert to be the main part of the trip. Most tours give you one night in the Sahara. This one gives you three nights beside the Erg Chebbi dunes, so you can ride a camel at sunset, add a quad or buggy ride, take a 4x4 trip with the nomads and still have time to rest.

You cross the High Atlas and visit Ouarzazate, Ait Ben Haddou and the Dades Valley on the way out, and return through Fes, with a night in the old imperial city. Book your private tour: tell us your dates and group size and we send a free quote with no obligation. For a shorter trip see our 5-day desert tour.

Itinerary

Day by day itinerary

A typical 6-day round trip from Marrakech. Stops and timings are adapted to you.

Day1

Marrakech to the Dades Valley

About 330 km

  • Leave Marrakech in the morning with your private driver and local guide.
  • Cross the High Atlas by the Tizi n'Tichka pass, with stops for photos.
  • Visit Ait Ben Haddou, the UNESCO-listed ksar of red-earth towers.
  • Stop in Ouarzazate, the gateway to the Sahara, and continue to the Dades Valley.
  • Dinner and night in the Dades Valley.

The long drive is split with stops for lunch and photos. The place for the night is confirmed in your free quote.

Day2

Dades Valley to Merzouga

About 300 km

  • Take a short walk or a drive in the Dades Valley and its rock formations.
  • Visit the Todra Gorges, a canyon with tall rock walls near Tinghir.
  • Drive to Merzouga across the desert plains of the south-east.
  • Ride a camel at sunset in the Erg Chebbi dunes.
  • Dinner, music and a night in a desert camp beside the dunes.

Camel ride timing depends on the season and your arrival time.

Day3

A full day in the Sahara

Merzouga

  • Watch the sunrise over the dunes and have breakfast at camp.
  • Spend the day in the desert at your own pace: walk the dunes, visit the Gnawa village of Khamlia or rest.
  • Add an activity if you wish: a camel trek, a quad or buggy ride or a 4x4 trip with the nomads.
  • Dinner and a second night in the camp.

Activities are optional and the prices are confirmed on request. Tell us what you would like.

Day4

A second full day in the Sahara

Merzouga

  • Watch the sunrise over the dunes and have breakfast at camp.
  • Spend the day at your own pace: a camel trek, a quad or buggy ride, a 4x4 trip with the nomads, or a long rest at camp.
  • Visit the village of Khamlia, known for its Gnawa music, or the market town of Rissani if you like.
  • Dinner and a third night in the camp.

Activities are optional and the prices are confirmed on request. Tell us what you would like.

Day5

Merzouga to Fes

About 460 km

  • Watch the sunrise over the dunes and have breakfast at camp.
  • Leave Merzouga and drive north along the Ziz Valley towards the Middle Atlas.
  • Stop for lunch near Midelt or in the cedar forest area.
  • Arrive in Fes in the evening.

This is a long driving day. We plan stops to rest, and the arrival time depends on traffic and the stops you choose.

Day6

Fes to Marrakech

About 530 km

  • Visit the medina of Fes el-Bali with a local guide in the morning if you like, or leave early.
  • Drive south through the Middle Atlas, with a lunch stop on the way.
  • Arrive in Marrakech in the evening, and the tour ends.

This is a long driving day. Arrival time depends on traffic and the stops you choose.

Good to know

Tips for your trip

  • The 6-day tour has two full days in the desert with no driving, and the last two days are long drives.
  • Spring and autumn are the most comfortable seasons. Summer is very hot and winter desert nights are cold.
  • Bring light layers, a warm jacket for the desert nights, a scarf, sunglasses, sun cream and closed shoes.
  • A camel trek, a quad or buggy ride or a 4x4 trip with the nomads can be added on the free days. Ask us for details.

FAQ

Questions about the 6-day tour

About the trip, how to book it and who we are.

The tour

Is the 6-day Marrakech to Merzouga tour worth it?

Yes, if the desert is the main reason for your trip. The tour has three nights in a camp beside the dunes and two full days in the Sahara, plus the High Atlas, Ait Ben Haddou, the Dades Valley and a night in Fes.

How is the 6-day tour different from the 4-day and 5-day tours?

The 6-day tour has three nights at Merzouga and two full days in the desert, with no driving on those days. The 5-day tour has two nights and one full day, and the 4-day tour has one night.

What can I do during the two free days in the Sahara?

You can watch the sunrise over the dunes, ride a camel, add a quad or buggy ride, take a 4x4 trip with the nomads, visit the Gnawa village of Khamlia or simply rest at camp. Prices for activities are confirmed on request.

How much driving is there on the 6-day tour?

Marrakech is about 560 km from Merzouga. The 6-day tour splits the distance over several days and has two days with no driving, then drives on to Fes and back. The longest days are the first one, Merzouga to Fes and Fes to Marrakech.

Where do we sleep on the 6-day tour?

You spend three nights in a desert camp beside the Erg Chebbi dunes and two nights on the road, in the Dades Valley and in Fes. We confirm the places in your quote.

Can I add more days or change the route?

Yes. We can add a night, extend the trip to Fes or other cities, or change the stops. Tell us your dates and we send a free quote.

Booking

How do I book the 6-day Marrakech to Merzouga desert tour?

Send us your dates and number of travellers with the contact form, by WhatsApp or by email. We reply with a free, no-obligation quote and, once you are happy with it, we confirm the booking and the details of your trip.

How much does the 6-day tour cost?

The price depends on your dates, the number of travellers and the type of camp, so we quote on request. Quotes are free and come with no obligation.

Where does the tour start and end?

The tour starts and ends in Marrakech. We agree the pick-up point and time with you when we confirm your booking.
