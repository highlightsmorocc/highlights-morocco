8-Day Morocco Tour from Fes

A private 8-day tour that starts and ends in Fes: the Sahara at Merzouga, the Dades Valley, Marrakech, Casablanca, Rabat and Chefchaouen, with a driver and a local guide.

Tour overview

Fes, the Sahara and Marrakech in 8 days

The 8-day Morocco tour from Fes is a private round trip by Highlights Morocco Tours. It starts with a guided day in Fes, crosses the Middle Atlas to Merzouga for a night in a Sahara desert camp, follows the Dades Valley and Ait Ben Haddou to Marrakech, continues to Casablanca and Rabat, and returns to Fes through Chefchaouen, Volubilis and Meknes. It includes a private driver and a local guide who speaks English or Spanish. The price is on request, with a free quote and no obligation.

Starting in Fes puts the longest desert drive early in the trip. You spend your first day in the medina, head for the Sahara on day 2, cross the Atlas to Marrakech, and then come back north through the coast and the Rif, ending where you started.

It is a typical route, not a fixed script. We adapt the nights and stops to your dates, and we can add a night in Fes, Marrakech or the desert. For a shorter trip see our 3-day Marrakech to Fes tour.

Itinerary

Day by day itinerary

Day1

Fes: your first day

Fes el-Bali

  • Spend the day with a local guide in the medina of Fes el-Bali, a UNESCO World Heritage site (listed in 1981).
  • Visit the Chouara tanneries, the old madrasas and the souks.
  • Enjoy a traditional lunch and free time in the evening.
Day2

Fes to Merzouga

About 460 km

  • Leave Fes in the morning and cross the Middle Atlas, with its cedar forests.
  • Stop for lunch near Midelt and follow the Ziz Valley to the south-east.
  • Reach the Erg Chebbi dunes in the late afternoon and ride a camel at sunset.
  • Dinner, music and a night in a desert camp beside the dunes.
Day3

Merzouga to the Dades Valley

About 300 km

  • Watch the sunrise over the dunes and have breakfast at camp.
  • Drive west towards Tinghir and visit the Todra Gorges.
  • Continue along the Dades Valley, with its rock formations and kasbahs.
  • Dinner and night in the Dades Valley.
Day4

Dades Valley to Marrakech

About 330 km

  • Leave the Dades Valley after breakfast and drive west.
  • Stop in Ouarzazate and visit Ait Ben Haddou.
  • Cross the High Atlas by the Tizi n'Tichka pass.
  • Arrive in Marrakech in the evening.
Day5

A day in Marrakech

Marrakech

  • Explore the medina with a local guide: the Koutoubia minaret, the souks, a palace and Jemaa el-Fnaa.
  • Take a break in a traditional courtyard cafe.
  • Spend the evening in Jemaa el-Fnaa, with its food stalls and storytellers.
Day6

Marrakech to Rabat via Casablanca

About 320 km

  • Leave Marrakech in the morning and drive north.
  • Visit the Hassan II Mosque in Casablanca if the schedule allows.
  • Continue to Rabat and spend the night there.
Day7

Rabat to Chefchaouen

About 240 km

  • Visit the Hassan Tower and the Kasbah of the Udayas in the morning.
  • Drive north to Chefchaouen, the blue town of the Rif.
  • Walk the old town in the evening.
Day8

Chefchaouen to Fes via Volubilis and Meknes

About 300 km with stops

  • Enjoy the blue lanes of Chefchaouen in the morning.
  • Drive south and visit Volubilis, the Roman site listed by UNESCO in 1997.
  • Stop in Meknes, an imperial city with the Bab Mansour gate.
  • Arrive in Fes in the evening.

Why this route

The desert early, the coast later

Starting in Fes lets you do the long desert drive when you are fresh and keeps the cities for the second half of the trip.

The return passes through Casablanca, Rabat and Chefchaouen, so you see the coast, the capital and the blue town before closing the loop in Fes.

Good to know

Tips for your trip

  • The route has several long driving days. The longest are between Merzouga and Fes or Marrakech.
  • Spring and autumn are the most comfortable seasons. Summer is very hot in the south and winter desert nights are cold.
  • Bring light layers, a warm jacket for the desert night, a scarf, sunglasses, sun cream and closed shoes.
  • Want to slow down? Ask us to add a night in Fes, Marrakech or the desert.

Questions about the 8-day tour

About the trip, how to book it and who we are.

The tour

Is the 8-day Morocco tour from Fes worth it?

Yes, if you want to see the main regions of Morocco in one trip. In 8 days you can combine imperial cities, the Atlas, the Sahara and the coast, with a private driver and a local guide.

What is the best 8-day Morocco itinerary from Fes?

A popular 8-day itinerary from Fes goes Merzouga, the Dades Valley, Marrakech, Casablanca, Rabat and Chefchaouen, and back to Fes. It avoids repeating the same road and includes a night in the Sahara.

How much driving is there on this tour?

Morocco is big, so there are some long driving days, mostly between Merzouga and the cities. We plan stops for lunch and rest, and we can add nights to slow down.

Does the tour include the Sahara?

Yes. It includes a camel ride at sunset in the Erg Chebbi dunes and a night in a desert camp at Merzouga. The camel ride depends on the season and your arrival time.

Can I change the route or add days?

Yes. We adapt the stops and the number of nights to your dates and interests, and we can add a second night in the desert, in Fes or in Marrakech.

How long is the drive from Fes to Merzouga?

Fes is about 460 km from Merzouga, which takes most of a day with stops through the Middle Atlas and the Ziz Valley. We plan stops for lunch and rest.

Can I finish in Marrakech instead of Fes?

Yes. We can end the tour in Marrakech after the Dades Valley and drop the northern part, or adapt the route. Tell us your flights.

Booking

How do I book the 8-day Morocco tour from Fes?

Send us your dates and number of travellers with the contact form, by WhatsApp or by email. We reply with a free, no-obligation quote and, once you are happy with it, we confirm the booking and the details of your trip.

How much does the 8-day tour cost?

The price depends on your dates, the number of travellers and the type of camp, so we quote on request. Quotes are free and come with no obligation.

Where does the tour start and end?

The tour starts and ends in Fes. We agree the pick-up point and time with you when we confirm your booking.
