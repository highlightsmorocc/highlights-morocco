Highlights

What you can see from Casablanca

Ideas for your private tour from Casablanca. We adapt every route to your plans.

  • 01

    Hassan II Mosque

    One of the largest mosques in the world, built on the edge of the Atlantic Ocean, with a minaret that dominates the coastline.

  • 02

    The Atlantic corniche

    A seafront promenade with beaches, cafes and restaurants, ideal for a relaxed first or last day in Morocco.

  • 03

    The old medina and art deco centre

    Discover the traditional medina and the city's downtown, known for its early twentieth-century architecture.
