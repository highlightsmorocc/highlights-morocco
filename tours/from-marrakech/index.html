FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer private tours from Marrakech?

Yes. We offer private 2, 3 and 4-day Merzouga desert tours from Marrakech, with a private driver and a local guide. Our multi-day tours start from Marrakech, Casablanca, Fes, Rabat, Tangier, Errachidia.

How many days do I need for a Merzouga desert tour?

We offer 2, 3 and 4-day tours from Marrakech to Merzouga. The right length depends on how much time you have and how relaxed you want the pace to be.
